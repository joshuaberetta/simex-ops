# SIMEX ops: operator manual

For ExCon members who deliver the SIMEX injects, watch the team mailboxes, answer in character and grade the responses. The app is at **https://ops.simex.kobolabs.dev**; ExCon signs in there as `undac` (or their own staff login). Participants use SIMEX Mail at **https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev** (a webmail-style sign-in page, then their team's mailbox) with the team logins you hand out. The two addresses share one sign-in: a participant who signs in at ops. is sent on to mail., and ExCon pages opened on mail. go back to ops. All times are **Nairobi time**.

Read section 7, "Safety controls", before your first shift.

SIMEX ops and SIMEX Mail are open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0: every page of both links to the source code on GitHub (the footer in ops, the bottom of the folder list and the sign-in page in SIMEX Mail). Exercise content is never in the repository.

---

## 1. How it works in one minute

- **Injects.** Each email inject from the MEL is one row on the **Injects** tab, with a send time, a persona as sender (e.g. `ccdpr@simex.kobolabs.dev`), recipients, a subject, a body and attachments.
- **Delivery.** One switch in **Settings → Delivery channel** decides how injects reach the teams (the status bar shows it: *via email* / *via SIMEX Mail*):
  - **Email (Resend)**, the main flow: injects go to the participants' real addresses from the personas (`ccdpr@simex.kobolabs.dev`), and their replies come back into the app.
  - **SIMEX Mail**, the backup: injects land in the teams' mailboxes inside this site, a webmail client participants open in their browser. No real email is involved. Use it if email has problems (see 5.6). As in Gmail or Outlook, a return arrow before a conversation's subject in the list means the mailbox has replied since the last message it received. **Filters** above every list (every mailbox: teams, members, team leaders, the persona desk and ExCon's read-only views) show only the **Unread**, **Starred**, **Has files** or **Not replied** conversations (something came in, nothing sent since), each with how many; they combine, and stay on through search, paging and **j** / **k** (the persona desk has more, 5.10). A reply can be **popped out** (the arrow at the top right of the reply box) into a window at the bottom right, so the conversation scrolls behind it while it is written, and popped back in. Messages written in SIMEX Mail (by the teams, the team leaders and the persona desk) can have **formatting** (bold, italics, underline, colours, lists, quotes, links: the bar under the message, the **T** button shows or hides it) and **pictures in the text** (pasted, dropped or added with the picture button; big photos are scaled down and put in at most 600 pixels wide). **Click a picture** in the message to resize it: **Small**, **Medium**, **Large** or **Original size** in the bar over it, or drag the blue corner; **Remove** (or Delete) takes it out. The size goes out with the message, in SIMEX Mail and by email. Whatever is pasted is cleaned: only that formatting is kept, and pictures from the web are dropped. The text without formatting is what search, SIMEX AI, the reports and the Replies tab read; a picture in the text is not listed as an attachment nor read as a document. Answered by email, the formatting and pictures go out in the email (pictures inline). ExCon's own forms (Compose, Answer as on a reply's page, injects) are still plain text.
- **What's new.** Each new feature is announced in the notifications drawer of everyone it concerns: ExCon's **Notifications** panel, the **megaphone** in SIMEX Mail (for the teams, the team leaders and the persona desk, where it sits beside the desk's own notifications bell so the star and the bell's count never overlap) and the bell on the role players' pages. Something new puts a small star on it (or on **Notifications**); it goes once the drawer has been opened, and the note stays listed. The drawer never opens by itself. The notes are written in the code (`ops/whatsnew.py`).
  - **Per inject**: each inject's own channel field decides (mixing both).
- **When.** At each inject's time the scheduler delivers it, but only if **live sending is armed** and nothing is paused. One inject usually becomes one message per team.
- **Answers.** Participants reply to (or write to) personas from SIMEX Mail. Every answer appears on the **Replies** tab, matched exactly to the inject and team it answers. You read it, answer in character, and grade it against the inject's rubric.
- **Nothing reaches participants by accident:**
  - live sending is off until someone arms it;
  - **Pause all sending** stops everything at once;
  - an inject that missed its time is held as **overdue**, never delivered late on its own.

## 2. The tabs

The tabs come in two groups: what you use while the exercise runs (**Injects**, **Replies**, **Team inboxes**, **Compose**, **Dashboard**, **Log**, **Role play**), then, after the divider, setting it up (**People & logins**, **Senders**, **Test runs**, **Settings**).

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Injects** | The schedule, grouped by day (click a day to fold it; each shows its count, time span, statuses, files and how many need attention). Filter by day, status or **Needs attention**; open an inject to edit, preview, test or send it; change the channel in bulk. Import from the MEL is here too, **Draft with AI** (5.8) and the **Library** of the participants' documents for suggested replies (5.12). |
| **Replies** | Everything participants sent: team summary, inject × team grid, **Read / opens & clicks**, the inbox, and grading. |
| **Team inboxes** | Each team's mailbox at a glance (received, unread, sent, last sign-in), **View as team**, the team leader's and each member's own mailbox, **Open the persona desk** (5.10), and **Clear mailboxes** after testing (6.2). |
| **Compose** | Write as any persona (but the team leader persona) to teams, team members or team leaders, now, without making an inject: the persona desk's **Write as a persona** on these screens (5.3), with what was sent from here and the desk below. **Invite to a meeting** puts a meeting on a persona's calendar with invitations to the teams, members or leaders ticked (5.14). |
| **Dashboard** | The exercise at a glance: per team, replies, response times, scores, sentiment over time and a funnel; the rubric heatmap; flags, possibly AI-written replies and documents, what teams ask for and commit to; an AI-written exercise summary (5.7). **Network**: a live map of who writes to whom, also as a wall screen and as a tab of the persona desk (5.7). **End-of-exercise reports** for each team and for ExCon (6.1). |
| **Log** | Every delivery the app made, with content and status, plus the activity log of every action. A delivery sent by mistake can be withdrawn from SIMEX Mail on its page (5.3). |
| **Role play** | The role players' schedule: every sitting of every role play (face to face, phone, VTC) by time, who plays it, where and for which team, **In position** / **Done**, **Slip** the schedule, checks, each role play's script and files, the import, the role players' briefing and FAQ, and printable run sheets (5.11). |
| **People & logins** | Teams, participants, and their logins (create, reset password, disable, printable credential sheet). |
| **Senders** | The personas: username (`cnn`), display name ("CNN Breaking News"); import or download them as a spreadsheet. Participants write to them (their address book grows with use). |
| **AI personas** | Each persona's profile (who they are, how they write, what they know), which shapes **Suggest a reply** and **ChatSIMEX** on the persona desk; and personas that answer the teams on their own, in character, while Settings → AI personas is on (5.9). |
| **Test runs** | Replays injects on a fast clock into the **Test team** mailbox (and, for email injects, to a test address). |
| **Settings** | **Delivery channel**, **Simulation clock**, footer, subject prefix and inject code in the subject, **AI analysis** and **AI instructions**, team-to-team messaging, attachment limit, email options, grace period, **live sending switch**, backup and restore, and **Start afresh** before going live (6.2). |
| **Help** | This manual (top right, next to your name). |

The status bar under the tabs shows the Nairobi clock, **Live disarmed** (green) or **LIVE ARMED** (red), **ALL SENDING PAUSED** when paused, when the scheduler last ran (red if it stopped), the next inject due, counts of overdue and failed injects, and the **Pause all sending** button.

## 3. Before the exercise (the day before at the latest)

### 3.1 Load the injects
1. In the knowledge base: `python3 -I scripts/export_inject_bundle.py`. It writes `work/simex-ops/injects.json` and `attachments.zip`.
2. **Injects → Import / update**, upload `injects.json` (or `MEL-MIL.xlsx`) and click **Check file**.
3. Read the summary (new / updated / with local edits / unchanged), then **Apply import**. New injects are **in-app**.
4. On the same page, upload `attachments.zip`. Files are matched to injects by the names in the MEL's *Supporting documents* column. Matching runs in the background once the upload has finished: the page shows its progress (*matching … of … injects*) and then how many files were attached and which matched no inject. You can leave the page meanwhile. If it shows *interrupted* (the app restarted, e.g. a deploy), upload the zip again: files already attached are replaced, not duplicated.
5. Open a few injects: documents the MEL lists but that are still missing are shown in orange under **Attachments**. Upload those by hand (max 30 MB each; put a link in the body for the 1-20 briefing video).

**Start over** (bottom of the same page): delete all attachments (to load a new zip), or all injects with their attachments, rubrics, grades and test-run items (to import the MEL from scratch). Type DELETE to confirm. Deliveries, inbox mail and replies stay, no longer linked to an inject. Download a backup first (Settings).

Only participant-facing fields are imported. Expected outcomes, story, theme, key figures and the ExCon description never enter the app. Re-importing updates what changed in the MEL, keeps your edits (unless you tick "Overwrite my local edits", which only overwrites fields the MEL itself changed) and never touches what was already delivered. A new inject is **scheduled** only if it has a send date *and* a readable time still in the future; one with no time ("TBC") or a time already past comes in as a **draft** for you to time and schedule. An import that fails part-way changes nothing.

**When the MEL changes** (as often as you like, also during the exercise): download the sheet again and repeat steps 2–3. Injects are matched by **Inject #**, so ask the MEL authors never to renumber or reuse one (a renumbered inject arrives as a new one, and the old one stays).
- A changed **send time** also fixes the status: a draft that had no time becomes **scheduled**, an **overdue** inject moved to a later time is **scheduled** again, and an inject whose time was removed goes back to **draft**. The check page shows these as e.g. *overdue → scheduled*. A draft you unscheduled yourself stays a draft.
- Injects that are **no longer in the file** are listed under *Not in this file*. They are still sent unless you tick **Cancel the … unsent inject(s) that are no longer in this file** before applying. Injects you created here (not imported) and injects already sent are never cancelled.

### 3.2 Teams, participants and logins
**People & logins.** A new app has placeholder teams A–D (with placeholder people, no logins) and the **Test team**. **People** comes first, grouped by team (then the persona desk and anyone without a team): each team's heading shows its members, its leader, how many logins are shared or not, who has no personal email or no login, its sign-in slips, and **Email logins not shared yet** for that team. Click a heading to fold the team (remembered in your browser); **Expand all / Collapse all** above. Teams and Team roles are below.

**Who has signed in.** Each heading also counts how many have signed in at least once and how many are **online** (seen in the last 5 minutes). In each person's row, next to their username: **online**, **seen … ago** (their latest page in SIMEX Mail or ops), **signed in** with the date, or **never signed in**, plus **n failed** after wrong passwords since their last good sign-in. A sign-in lasts 14 days on a device, so watch *seen* rather than *signed in*. Click it for that person's **activity** page: last sign-in and last seen; every sign-in (with the password or the QR code), sign-out and failed sign-in with the device and address it came from; what they did in SIMEX Mail (searches, labels, archive, drafts, sends), what they opened and downloaded, and what they wrote, latest first (filter by kind; each opened or sent item links to its delivery or reply). On the side, the devices they signed in on and those with alerts on. ExCon's own logins aren't tracked here. The sign-in log stays through **Start afresh** (like the logins); the SIMEX Mail activity goes with the mailboxes.

**Team members and team leaders.** Each team has **team members**, the participants (its focal point, or a few people sharing the team's one mailbox), and **one team leader**: an ExCon person who monitors the team, is not part of it, and plays the team leader persona (3.3). A team can't have two leaders, and a leader needs a team.

**Team roles.** Members can also hold a function within their team, such as *Deputy Team Leader*, so that the Dashboard and the reports can show behaviour by role. Add the roles under **Team roles** (name and order; renaming one renames it for everyone holding it, deleting one leaves its members without a role), then pick each member's in their row in **People** (the select appears for team members only; team leaders, from ExCon, have none), or give it in the roster's role column. A team role changes nothing in SIMEX Mail: the member still shares the team's inbox like the others.

1. Paste the roster into **Paste a roster**, one line per person: `name,email,team code,role` (email optional for in-app; role `tl` for the team's leader, empty for members, or a member's team role such as `Deputy Team Leader`, added to Team roles if new). Keep **create logins** and **remove the placeholder people** ticked.
   ```
   Jane Doe,jane@example.org,a,tl
   John Roe,,a,Deputy Team Leader
   Ann Poe,,a
   ```
2. The page then shows **New logins: shown once**: username and password for each new person. Click **Printable sheet** and print it (one slip per person, with the SIMEX Mail address https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev and a **QR code**), or copy it somewhere safe. Passwords are not stored readable; if one is lost, use **reset password** on that person.
   - **QR sign-in.** Scanning a slip's QR code with a phone's camera signs that person in on the phone and opens **Set up this device**: one button to turn on alerts (on an iPhone, first **Add to Home Screen**; the home-screen app is then signed in already). A code works on any number of devices for 14 days from printing, or until that person's password is reset or their login disabled; it never signs in an ExCon login (persona desk logins get one). Treat a slip like the password: hand it to its person.
   - **Sign-in slips** prints slips again (QR code and username, passwords unchanged): for everyone (link above the People table), a team (**sign-in slips** on its row) or one person (**slip** next to their username). Use it the day before, so the codes are fresh.
3. Each team shares **one mailbox**: everyone in Team A sees Team A's mail, and what one member sends appears in everyone's *Sent*, with the sender's name. With **personal mailboxes** on (Settings, on by default) each member also has **My mail**, their own mailbox at `name@<team code>.teams.simex.kobolabs.dev` (or the address set on the Senders tab, 3.3): a copy of everything delivered to the team, plus an inject sent *one per participant* (theirs only, never in the team inbox), plus personal mail between teammates and with their team leader (To, Cc, Bcc, as in any webmail). Members choose the **From** address of each message (the team's or their own, colour-coded); what goes from the team's address is in the team inbox's Sent. A member's answer to a persona from their own address is also put in the team inbox's Sent, and is the reply ExCon sees and grades, as before; mail only between members never reaches the Replies tab. Reading a copy in My mail counts as the team reading the inject. The team leader writes to the team (team inbox and every My mail) or to chosen members only (their My mail); In the leader's mailbox, mail from the team is labelled **Team account** or **Personal account** by the address it came from. Members are alerted (and pushed) for team mail and personal mail alike, in either tab. ExCon and the leader read every member's My mail read-only (**Team inboxes** → the member's name; the leader's **Team members** tab).
4. **Team leaders** sign in at the same SIMEX Mail address and get five tabs: **My mail** (their own mailbox, as the team leader persona), the **team inbox** read-only (with SIMEX AI's reading of each reply from the team: who wrote it, tone, quality, flags, possibly AI-written), **Team members** (each member with their team role, what they have written and read, alerts, last sign-in, and their own mailbox read-only), **Team leaders** (the other team leaders' mailboxes, read-only, one per team in the side bar; never the other teams' inboxes) and the team's **Dashboard**, plus, while AI personas are on (5.9), **AI personas**: the team's conversations with them. In **My mail**, above a conversation where a member or the team wrote to them, they can ask for a **suggested reply** (5.12). They only ever see their own team's inbox, write only to their own team, and never see injects, rubrics or grades. Their guide is under **?** in SIMEX Mail. Changing someone's role takes effect on their next page; their devices then turn alerts on again.
5. **Personal emails and login emails.** Each person has, next to the email-channel address, a **personal email**: their own address outside the exercise. It receives their login email and the email copies of injects marked *also by email to personal addresses* (3.5); the email channel still uses the first address.
   - **Import a roster** (right column) takes a spreadsheet (.xlsx) or CSV with a header row `Name`, `Email` (the personal email), `Team` (`A` or `Team A`) and optionally `Role` (`tl`). **Check the roster** shows the plan before anything changes: who is matched (same email, same name, or a *similar name* in the same team, e.g. "Joel Cretet (Tony)" for "Joel Cretet": check those), what changes (personal email, team), who is added as a team member, lines skipped (an unusable address, no team) and who is here but not in the file (left as they are). **Apply** with **create logins** ticked gives the new people logins (shown once). Names, usernames and the email-channel address are never changed. New usernames are the team code, first and last name (`c.karina.avalos`). The roster paste takes a fifth column for the personal email too: `name,email,team code,role,personal email`.
   - **Email logins** (right column) emails each person, at their personal email, their username, a **new password**, the QR code and sign-in link of their slip, how to start (phone: scan, alerts, Home Screen on an iPhone; laptop: the address and password) and what their mailboxes are, plus an optional **note** (e.g. when and where the exercise starts). Choose everyone or a team; by default only those whose login isn't shared yet (tick **also those already shared** to send new passwords to them too). **Answers go to**: an ExCon address for their answers (remembered); left empty, answers land on the Replies tab, ignored. One person: **email login** in their row. Sending issues a new password, so their printed slips and old password stop working; the Log keeps each email without its password and link. The QR code and link work for 14 days from sending, the password until it is reset.
   - Each row shows **shared** (when, by email or by hand) or **not shared**, and *email failed* or a bounce. A slip handed out on paper: **mark shared** (**unmark** to undo). **Reset password** marks the login not shared, as nobody has the new one yet.
6. **Persona desk** (5.10): role-players who answer as the personas get the role **Persona desk (ExCon)** (no team; roster role `desk`, team code empty). They sign in at the SIMEX Mail address with their username and password (or scan the QR code on their slip or in their login email: it opens **Set up this device** for the persona desk's mailbox) and reach SIMEX Mail only, never these screens.
7. **Role players** (5.11): people who play characters face to face or by phone get the role **Role player (ExCon)** (no team; roster role `rp`). Most are added by the role-play import; give them logins here. Their QR code opens their role plays and turns on reminders on their phone; they reach nothing else.
8. Give yourself a login in the **Test team** (Add person → team "Test team" → create login) to try SIMEX Mail as a participant.

- **Team code** fills `{team}` in addresses (`undac-{team}-tl` → `undac-a-tl@…`); **team name** fills `{team_name}`.
- **Disable** blocks a login without deleting the person; **delete** removes both.
- More ExCon logins: Django admin (`/admin/`, staff users or the `excon` group).

### 3.3 Personas (Senders)
One row per persona, created from the MEL's sender aliases. Fix display names (e.g. `CCDPR` → "CCDPR Deputy Director"); **rename in injects** keeps injects in step when a username changes. Add personas the MEL lacks (e.g. `cnn` / "CNN Breaking News"). **Organisation (teams see it)** is the organisation a persona speaks for (e.g. *Ministry of Interior*): the teams see it beside the persona in their address book and in SIMEX Calendar (guests, invitations). **Category** and **organisation / use** stay ExCon's: never shown to the teams. The senders list import and export have the column *Organisation (shown to teams)*; a plain *Organisation* column is still read as ExCon's notes.

**Which mailbox.** SIMEX Mail names the mailbox at the top, next to the logo, each kind in its own colour: a team inbox (blue), a member's **My mail** (green), a team leader's mailbox and tabs (purple), the persona desk (teal), and ExCon's read-only views (amber, with *Back to ExCon*). The search box says which mailbox it searches.

Active senders are who participants can write to. Their **address book** starts empty and grows with use, as in a webmail: whoever wrote to their mailbox or was written to (a member's book is their own mailbox's and the team inbox's), latest first. To write to a persona first, a team types its full SIMEX Mail address (from an inject, a document, a signature): SIMEX Mail never lists the personas they haven't been in touch with. Teammates' and the team's addresses are found the same way. Team leaders' and the persona desk's books are full lists, as before (the desk's lists every team and every member). In SIMEX Mail a member is named with their team, e.g. **Team A (Jane Doe)**, as the From of what they send, in To and Cc and in the address book. Untick **active** to make a persona unreachable.

**The team leader persona.** Tick **team leader** on the persona each team's leader plays (`undac-{team}-tl`; the import ticks it for you). Then, for each team:
- what is sent as it (injects, follow-ups, your **Answer as** on the Replies tab, and what the leader writes) lands in the team's inbox as usual **and** in the leader's **Sent**, in the same conversation;
- what the team writes to it (or copies it on) is a reply as for any persona (Replies tab, AI analysis, grading) **and** arrives in the leader's **Inbox**;
- the leader's own answers to the team show on the reply's page as *answered by the team leader*, so check there before answering as the leader yourself.

**Address in SIMEX Mail.** Each persona has a **username** (what injects use, and its email address: `username@simex.kobolabs.dev`) and, for SIMEX Mail, any address you like: the username and domain after it on its row, e.g. `news` @ `cnn.com`. Empty parts default to the username and simex.kobolabs.dev. Participants see that address on messages and in their address book. Two personas can show the same name on different domains: `news@cnn.com` (username `cnn-news`) and `news@reuters.com` (username `reuters-news`); only the full SIMEX Mail address has to be unique.
**Team and member addresses.** Below the personas, set the SIMEX Mail addresses of the teams' shared inboxes and of members' own mailboxes (My mail) the same way, a username and a domain: `{team}` is the team code; for members `{name}` is the full name (`jane.doe`), `{first}` and `{last}` the first and last names. For example `undac-{team}` @ `epeigonia.org` gives Team A `undac-a@epeigonia.org`, and `{first}.{last}` @ `epeigonia.org` gives `jane.doe@epeigonia.org`. Empty parts use the defaults (`a@teams.simex.kobolabs.dev`, `jane.doe@a.teams.simex.kobolabs.dev`). A member whose address is taken already (a namesake in another team, a team or a persona) gets their number after it (`jane.doe.42@…`); a team address can't be a persona's, nor a persona's a team's. The table under the form shows each team's address and a few of its members'. SIMEX Mail only (nothing is emailed there); mail already sent keeps the address it had, and answering it still reaches the right mailbox. Set them before the exercise.
**Colour on the persona desk.** Each persona has a colour on the persona desk (5.10), picked from its username; choose another in its row (the select next to *rename in injects*) when two look alike.
By email every persona sends from **its username @simex.kobolabs.dev**, the only domain Resend can send from, so switching to email (5.6) changes the addresses teams see.
**Import a list of senders.** Under the Add row, upload a spreadsheet (.xlsx) or CSV with a header row: `Alias` (the address in SIMEX Mail, e.g. `who@epeigonia.org`), and optionally `Username` (else the alias's username, `who`), `Display name`, `Organisation / use`, `Category` and `Active` (yes / no). Organisation / use and category are ExCon's notes, shown and editable on each row, never to participants. Each row is the sender with that username (or, without a Username column, the one with that SIMEX Mail address, e.g. `news@cnn.com` for `cnn-news`); the others are added. The check page shows every row as **add**, **update** (only fills in empty values, or gives a persona with the default address the file's), **changes** (amber: it would replace a name, address, note or category set here; each such value is marked *replaces*, old → new), unchanged or **skipped** (a bad address, one another persona or a team already has, a line repeated). Untick a row to leave that sender alone. Senders active here but not in the file are listed: tick one to make it inactive (never the team leader persona). Empty cells never clear a value and usernames are never renamed (use the row's username field and *rename in injects*). **Download the senders** gives the same columns, to edit and import again.
When you import the MEL, the check page offers to give senders without a domain the address in the MEL's sender column (ticked; it lists them). If the MEL uses one name on several domains (`news@cnn.com`, `news@reuters.com`), they become separate personas, `cnn-news` and `reuters-news`. An address you set here is never changed by an import.

### 3.4 Settings
- **Default footer:** "EXERCISE EXERCISE EXERCISE …" at the bottom of every message.
- **Subject prefix** (e.g. `[SIMEX] `) and **put the inject code in the subject**: every inject's subject then starts with its code, e.g. `[1-15] Request for assessment`, so teams and ExCon can refer to it. Off by default; it applies to injects delivered after you turn it on (both channels).
- **AI analysis:** off by default; see 5.7.
- **Teams may write to other teams:** off by default (teams write only to personas). Turn it on if the scenario wants OSOCC-to-OSOCC traffic; those messages show under Team inboxes but are not "replies".
- **Personal mailboxes:** on by default. Each member also has their own mailbox (**My mail**) next to the team inbox (3.2). Off: only the team inbox, as before; the My mail copies already made stay but aren't shown.
- **Max attachments per message:** what participants may attach (default 10 MB).
- **Email channel:** reply-to (leave empty), ExCon Bcc, test addresses. Only matters for injects on the email channel. Meeting invitations from the persona desk go by email too, as calendar invitations (5.14).
- **Grace period:** 10 minutes by default (see 7.3).
- **Calendar → Remind before an event:** 10 minutes by default: SIMEX Calendar's reminders and ExCon's notice for a persona's meeting nobody on the desk has (5.14); 0 turns them off.
- **Persona desk → show the injects to the persona desk:** on by default: the desk's **Injects** tab (5.10), read-only, with the injects still to go. Off: the tab is gone.
- **Persona desk → Notify ExCon after:** 20 minutes by default: a notification when a team has waited that long for a persona's answer on the persona desk (5.10), once per message waiting; 0 turns it off.
- **Simulation clock:** off by default. On, SIMEX Mail dates every message in the scenario's timeline and shows a calendar and clock of the simulation date and time at the top. **Team members see only the simulation time; team leaders, the persona desk and ExCon see both** (the real time, with the simulation time under it, marked with a small clock; the top shows both clocks). The ops status bar shows it too, next to the real time: a calendar tile with the simulation time and date (and its speed when it isn't 1×), ticking and kept in step when the clock follows an inject, or *Simulation clock off*; click either to come here.
  - **The simulation time now:** set it to start the clock (the link under it fills in the injects' earliest Scenario time); change it at any time to jump the clock. Saving the card without touching it leaves the clock running as it is.
  - **Speed:** 1 = real time, 4 = four scenario minutes per real minute, 0 = the clock stands still (to pause it between days).
  - **Follow the injects:** a live inject delivered with a later **Scenario time** than the clock moves the clock forward to it (never back, as injects aren't always in scenario order).
  - An inject's delivery is dated at its own **Scenario time** (when it has one); everything else (follow-ups, answers, what the teams and the desk write) at the clock's time when it arrives. A message keeps its date: changing the clock later doesn't re-date mail already in the mailboxes. Mail from before the clock was on, or while it's off, shows the real time. Quoted text in replies and forwards uses the simulation date. Email isn't affected (the mail client sets the date).

### 3.5 Go through the injects
Start with **Needs attention** ticked: it lists only the unsent injects with something to fix, each with tags under its subject (hover a tag for the detail):
- red: **N docs missing** (named in the MEL's *Supporting documents* but not attached; upload them on the inject page or remove the line), **no sender**, **no subject**, **no body** (and nothing attached), **failed**;
- yellow, to look at: **no attachment** (the text says *attached* but nothing is), **unknown sender**, **placeholder** (XXX, [INSERT …], TBC, a list of team slots to cut down), **ExCon note** (`***` left in the body), **subject is a file name**, **no send time**;
- purple **AI: …**: what SIMEX AI found reading the inject (section 5.7), e.g. a weekday that doesn't match the date, a greeting that doesn't fit the recipients, a document the text mentions that isn't attached.

The inject page lists the same points under **Before sending**. If an AI point is wrong or intended, **Dismiss the AI points** hides them from the list until the inject changes. Sent and cancelled injects are never flagged. Folded days are remembered in your browser; **Expand all / Collapse all** are in the bar, and **all** selects only the injects of open days.

Then open each one:
- **Send at:** the real (Nairobi) time; the MEL's scenario time is separate.
- **Channel:** set for all injects by Settings → Delivery channel; the inject page shows which one applies. Only in *Per inject* mode can you choose here.
- **Sender username / name**, **Recipients** (*one per team*, *one per participant* — lands in that person's own mailbox (**My mail**) only, so the team inbox doesn't fill with one copy per member; with personal mailboxes off, in the team inbox addressed to them; reading or answering it counts for the team as usual, and **View as team** and the leader's team inbox don't show it (open the member's mailbox) —, *everyone*), teams to leave out, and **also to team leaders**: a copy in each team leader's **My mail** (by email: a copy to the leader's address). The import ticks it when the MEL's recipients name the TL ("Team members & TL"); the Injects list shows *+ team leaders*. An inject from the team leader persona is in the leader's Sent anyway. A follow-up reaches the leaders' copies too. A leader who answers their email copy is kept on the Replies tab as *from the team leader* and never counts as the team's reply.
- **Also by email to personal addresses** (while Settings → Delivery channel isn't *Email*): delivered in SIMEX Mail as usual **and** emailed by Resend to each recipient's personal email (3.2): the team's members (one email each, so their addresses stay private; *everyone* and *only the addresses below* as one email with every participant in Bcc), and the team leaders when *also to team leaders* is ticked. The inject's extra To, Cc and Bcc are not used. People without a personal email are listed in the preview's warnings. An answer to the email copy comes into the Replies tab like any email reply (matched to the inject and the person). Test sends and test runs leave the email copies out; the preview shows them. The Injects list shows *+ email*.
- **Subject and body:** plain text; `{team}`, `{team_name}`, `{name}`, `{first_name}` are filled per message.
- **Preview** (right): exactly what each team gets. **Clear every orange warning.**
- **A different version per team** (e.g. 1-12, a letter of attestation for each team): on the inject page, **Duplicate** → *Copy for* **Team A only**, with **and take that team off 1-12** ticked, then **Duplicate inject**. The copy, **1-12-A**, is a draft for Team A only, with the same send time, files and rubric; 1-12 no longer goes to Team A. Change the copy (swap its attachment, edit the body), **Save** and **Schedule** it, and repeat for the other teams (from 1-12 each time). *The same teams as this one* makes a plain copy (1-12.2). Copies are never touched by a re-import of the MEL, and each copy counts as its own inject on the Replies tab and in the reports. The Injects list names the teams each inject goes to in the **To** column (*All teams* when none are left out).
- **Replies & rubric:** tick "teams are expected to reply", set "within (minutes)", add criteria with max points (or copy another inject's rubric). Tick **critical** for a key inject, and **must-have** for a criterion a team must not miss: in the end-of-exercise reports (6.1) a critical inject with no reply in time, or a must-have criterion graded at a third of its points or less, is listed as a *critical point missed*. Set them before the exercise.

Bulk: tick injects in the list and use **Schedule / Back to draft / Cancel / Shift by N min / Channel: In-app | Email / Personal email: Also email | SIMEX Mail only** (the Channel buttons appear only in *Per inject* mode; otherwise Settings decides for all injects; the Personal email ones unless the channel is *Email*).

### 3.6 Prepare the backup channel
Even if you plan to use email, make SIMEX Mail ready so you can switch in a minute:
1. **People & logins:** check every participant is in the right team and create their logins (one by one or by pasting the roster). Passwords are shown once: print the **credentials sheet** and keep it with ExCon.
2. Sign in once as a test participant (or use **Team inboxes → View as team**) to see what teams would see.
3. Note the address to give teams: **https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev** (they land in their mailbox after signing in).
4. Hand out the slips and have everyone **scan the QR code** with their phone: it signs them in and walks them through alerts, including, on an iPhone, adding SIMEX Mail to the home screen (only then do iPhones get notifications with SIMEX Mail closed). On a laptop they sign in with the username and password and click **Turn on alerts**.

### 3.7 Rehearse (at least once) — section 4.
End the rehearsal as you will end the exercise: grade the Test team's replies, then compile its report (**Dashboard → End-of-exercise reports → Test team**, 6.1) to check the figures and see what the write-up looks like. Clear the Test team's mailboxes afterwards (6.2); when every rehearsal is done, **Settings → Start afresh** (6.2) so the exercise starts with empty mailboxes, Dashboard and Log.

### 3.8 Take a backup
**Settings → Download backup.** One file with everything: injects, attachments, mailboxes, logins, grades.
Late in the exercise it can be several hundred MB (every file participants sent is in it): download it on a good connection. The database also has DigitalOcean's own daily backups.

## 5. During the exercise

### 5.1 Start of each day
1. Status bar: the scheduler ran seconds ago (if it says "is the worker running?", see 8).
2. **Injects** for today: everything that should go out is **scheduled**; shift times if the exercise runs late.
3. If you deliver by SIMEX Mail: hand out (or re-check) logins; teams sign in at **https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev** and keep the tab open.
4. **Settings → Live sending:** type `SEND LIVE`, **Arm live sending**.

### 5.2 Watching deliveries
- **scheduled → sending → sent**. In-app deliveries are instant; **partial** / **failed** mean some or all deliveries failed (in-app this is rare: usually a team without an inbox).
- **Team inboxes** shows what each team has received and whether they have read it.
- **Replies → Read / opens & clicks:** for in-app, *read* = someone in the team opened the conversation (with the time to first read and how often it was opened), *downloads* = attachments downloaded. For email, Resend's opens and clicks (a lower bound) and bounces.

### 5.3 Sending by hand, holding, changing
- **Send now:** on the inject, type its id under **Send live now**. In-app messages land at once.
- **Hold:** **Back to draft** or **Cancel inject**. **Edit:** change and **Save** — what is saved at the send time is what goes out.
- **Ad-hoc inject:** **Injects → New inject**: any persona to any teams, participants or (email) addresses. It starts a new conversation; use it for new information, not to correct one already sent.
- **Just a message:** **Compose** writes as any persona but the team leader persona to the teams, members and team leaders you tick, at once, with no inject (as **Write as a persona** on the persona desk, 5.10): one message each, in SIMEX Mail or by email as the delivery channel is, logged as kind *desk* (kept out of the injects' figures; the teams' replies to it are matched like any other). **To other personas** sends it as persona mail too (5.10): on the persona desk for them, an AI persona answering on its own; with no team ticked, only the desk sees it. **Cc other personas** shows the personas ticked on each team's message, as on the desk, so a team's reply all reaches them too. A member's goes to their own mailbox only. A team leader's (e.g. **Team A leader (Jane Doe)**) goes to the leader's own mailbox only, never the team's (by email, to the leader's email address). SIMEX Mail doesn't let a leader write to personas, so they can only read it there; by email, their answer comes into the Replies tab as *from the team leader*. Live sending must be armed (but for the Test team) and sending not paused. Below the form: everything sent from Compose and the desk, with who sent it. Its conversations show on the persona desk, where you answer the teams' replies.
- **Follow-up / correction** (a document or link was missing, a figure was wrong): on the delivered inject, **Send a follow-up**. Tick who gets it (every recipient of the inject is ticked; untick the teams that don't need it), keep the subject `Re: {subject}` ({subject} is the original subject, so mail clients group it with the original), write the message (placeholders {team} {team_name} {name} {first_name} work), tick attachments of the inject or add new files (they are also added to the inject), type the inject id and **Send follow-up**. It comes from the inject's persona in the team's existing conversation (in-app, delivered at once) or as a reply in the email thread (email, sent by the scheduler within seconds, quoting the original). It is a live send: live sending must be armed and sending not paused. Teams' replies to it count as replies to the inject, and it shows in the conversation on the reply page and in the Log (kind *followup*).
- **Let SIMEX AI write it:** a new inject, a follow-up or a persona's answer can be drafted with AI first (5.8); you still send it here.
- **Sent by mistake?** Open the delivery in the **Log** (or from **Deliveries for this inject** on the inject page) and use **Withdraw from SIMEX Mail**. Tick the teams to take it back from (it lists each team's copies: the team inbox, members' and the leader's own, the persona desk's), type `WITHDRAW` and click **Withdraw**. Every copy in those teams' mailboxes is deleted, including the team leader's copy of an inject sent *also to team leaders*. The delivery stays in the Log as *withdrawn*: out of the delivery and read figures, and never followed up. Teams' replies to it stay on the Replies tab. Anyone who already opened it, or got the alert on their phone, has seen it. An email that went out can't be recalled; only the SIMEX Mail copies go. To send a corrected version to the team, **Duplicate** the inject for that team (3.5), fix the copy and send it; sending the inject itself again (**Back to draft**, then send) goes to all its teams once more.
- **Not sent yet?** Just fix the inject (add the attachment, edit the body) and **Save**; no follow-up needed.
- **Stop everything:** **Pause all sending**; **Resume sending** continues without duplicates.

### 5.4 Replies and grading
- **Replies tab** (refreshes every minute): the **Teams** table, the **Injects × teams** grid (green replied, orange late, red overdue with no reply, blue waiting), **Read / opens & clicks**, and the **Inbox** of everything teams sent to personas.
  - In-app replies are matched **exactly** to the message they answer. A *new* message a team writes to a persona is matched to that persona's latest message to the team and marked **guess**; check it. **unmatched** needs assigning.
  - Out-of-office and other automatic answers to an email inject are kept but set to **ignored** (they don't count, get no AI persona answer and no AI analysis); set the Status back if one was a real reply.
- **Inbox → Conversations** (the default) shows one row per back-and-forth: an in-app conversation, or for email the same team, persona and subject. It gives the latest message, how many the team sent and how many were answered, and **awaiting answer** when the team wrote last and no persona has answered since. **Messages** lists every message on its own, as before.
- **A reply's page** shows the whole conversation in order: the inject (or message) that started it, each message from the team and each persona answer. The highlighted one is the message the page is about; click another to open it. **Answer as <persona>** always answers the latest message, so the conversation stays in one thread.
- **Files** show under the message they came with (the team's, the inject's or a persona answer's): click the name to download. Photos and screenshots (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP) are previewed in the page; click one to open it full size. A file too large to keep shows as *too large, not stored*.
- On that page you can also read it, **view in the team's mailbox**, correct the **Match**, set **Status** (ignored replies don't count), and **Answer as <persona>**: the answer lands in the team's mailbox in the same conversation (or goes by email for email threads), and their next reply is tracked too. **Attach files** adds files from your computer, and **Attach documents of <inject>** any of the matched inject's documents (up to 28 MB in all). An answer can be just files.
- **Grading:** from a grid cell or **Grade …**: all of the team's replies to that inject together; a score per criterion, comment, name; **Save and next team**. Non-responses can be graded too. **Export grades (CSV)** for the debrief.
- **Grade as you go, with the reports in mind:** the team's end-of-exercise report is written from your grades and comments, so write a comment as you would say it to the team. Answer teams from the reply page (or an AI draft) rather than outside the app: the reports count what teams asked for and how fast it was answered.

Write rubrics as observable behaviour ("acknowledged within 30 min", "named the cluster lead"), 2–4 points each. Nothing is copied from the MEL's expected outcomes.

**Notifications.** **Notifications** in the top bar (with the number you haven't read) opens a panel on the right listing what has happened, newest first: new replies (✉) and teams waiting too long for a persona's answer on the persona desk (5.10), AI flags (⚑ *needs ExCon attention*, *safety*), injects that failed, were only partly delivered or went overdue (⚠), and finished jobs and system news (⚙: an AI draft ready, the exercise summary or the end-of-exercise reports written, inject documents imported, the scheduler stopped). Click one to open it (it is then read); **✕** dismisses it. The buttons at the top show only replies, flags, delivery or system news; **Mark all read** and **Clear all** act on what is shown. Read and dismissed are yours alone: each ExCon login has its own list, the same on every computer it uses. Esc or a click elsewhere closes the panel; **📌** keeps it open beside the page as you move between tabs (click it again, or ✕ at the top, to unpin). Notifications are kept even while no ExCon page is open, so nothing is missed overnight.

**Alerts without refreshing.** Every ExCon page checks for news every 10 seconds. Something new shows a short toast at the bottom right while the panel is closed (click it to open it), plays a chime (not for finished jobs), updates the **Notifications** and **Replies** badges and the tab title and icon, and shows a desktop notification while the tab is in the background. **🔔 Alerts** in the status bar turns the sound and desktop notifications on or off; click it once on each computer to allow desktop notifications. Open tabs share what has been seen, so an alert comes once however many ExCon pages are open. Participants get the same kind of alerts in SIMEX Mail (a chime, a blue bar, the tab badge and a desktop notification once they click **Turn on alerts**; the speaker at the top right mutes them, and on the persona desk the bell next to it is the desk login's own notifications). Their notifications are also *pushed*: a device that turned on alerts is told about each new message even with SIMEX Mail closed (on an iPhone, only from the home-screen app). They show the sender and subject, nothing more. **Sign out** stops them on that device (so a shared phone or laptop never shows the last team's mail); signing in again turns them back on. Pushes need the `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` secret; without it, alerts only work while SIMEX Mail is open.

### 5.5 End of each day
**Settings → Disarm** (unless injects run overnight), **Download backup**, deal with overdue or failed injects, and grade the day's replies while they are fresh (the reports use the grades, 6.1).

### 5.6 Switching to SIMEX Mail when email has problems
Switch when teams don't receive emails (spam folders, bounces, the venue network blocking mail, Resend down or out of quota) and it can't be fixed quickly.
1. **Pause all sending** (top right) while you decide, so nothing half-goes.
2. **Settings → Delivery channel → SIMEX Mail**, type `SWITCH`, **Apply**. The status bar now says *via SIMEX Mail*.
3. Tell the teams (in person or by phone): open **https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev** and sign in with the login on their credentials sheet.
4. Re-deliver what they missed: open the injects that went out by email but didn't arrive, **Back to draft** if they show *sent*, then **Send now** — they now land in the team mailboxes. (Or select them in the list and **Schedule** with a new time.)
5. **Resume sending.** Everything not yet sent goes to the mailboxes from now on.

Replies to emails sent earlier keep arriving on the Replies tab. To go back to email, switch the channel back the same way. **Per inject** mode lets you mix: e.g. personal emails (attestations) by email, everything else in-app.

### 5.7 AI analysis and the Dashboard
When **Settings → AI analysis** is on, SIMEX AI reads what teams send. It is for ExCon only: participants never see any of it, and it never changes a grade.
- **Each reply** (SIMEX AI, fast model, within a minute of arriving): a one-line summary, tone and sentiment, clarity / completeness / actionability (1–5), what the team asks for and commits to, the figures it states, flags (*misreads the inject*, *needs ExCon attention*, *safety or duty-of-care issue*, *sent to the wrong persona*, *steps out of the exercise*, *no action or decision*), and how likely the text is **AI-written** (0–100 %). It shows on the reply's page under **AI analysis**, with **Re-run**.
- **Each team's thread on an inject with a rubric** (SIMEX AI, deep model, once the thread has been quiet for 3 minutes, and again if the team writes more): suggested points per criterion with a reason and a quote from the reply. On the grade page each criterion shows **AI n** with the reason; **Fill in the AI suggestions** copies them into the form, and nothing is saved until you click **Save**. The grid on the Replies tab shows *AI n / max* under the grade.
- **Each document a team sends** (SIMEX AI, fast model, within a minute): every file a member or the team leader attaches, in SIMEX Mail to anyone (personas, teammates, the leader, another team) or to an email to a persona: Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, PDF, text files and photos. Files already sent before it was turned on are read too, oldest first. Forwarded inject documents and files from personas or ExCon aren't the team's and are left out; each file is read once per team, however many mailboxes it sits in. SIMEX AI says what it is (*project plan*, *situation report*…), whether it answers the inject, and how likely it was written with an **AI assistant** (0–100 %), with the passages that read most like AI. Besides the text, it is given what the file records about itself, shown as **What the file records**: the application, the author, when it was created and last saved against when it was sent, and for Word the editing time and the number of editing sessions (a 2,000-word plan with 3 minutes of editing in one session was most likely pasted in). Old Word/Excel (.doc, .xls), zips, videos, PDFs over 100 pages and images over 5 MB aren't read (*not read*, with the reason). On a reply's page each file shows its reading (or **Analyse document**); **Dashboard → Documents teams sent** lists those at 60 % and above, and **all … read** opens the list of every file with its reading; a file's page has **Download** and **Re-run**. A document read as *likely AI-assisted* (70 % and above) raises a notification, at most one per team per hour.
- **Team leaders** see SIMEX AI's reading of their own team's replies and documents (not the grade suggestions, not other teams): in the team inbox under each message and on their Dashboard tab in SIMEX Mail.
- **AI meter in the mail lists and on each message:** a conversation holding a message the team wrote, or a file it sent, that SIMEX AI has read shows a meter on its row with the highest estimate (**AI 47%**), from green (probably the team's own) through amber (possibly AI-assisted, 40 % and above) to red (likely, 70 % and above); hover it for the reason. Opening the conversation, each such message has the same meter in its header (the higher of the message's and its files' estimates), and the reading under it gives each file its own. Team leaders see it in their team inbox and their members' mailboxes; ExCon sees it, with the reading under each message, when opening a team's mailboxes from Team inboxes (`/inbox/as/…`). Members, other teams' leaders and the persona desk never see it.
- **"AI-written"** is a hint to look closer, never proof: it is judged from the style of the text and how fast it was written after the team opened the inject (for documents, also what the file records about its editing). Short messages and non-native English make it unreliable; filled-in templates, figures pasted from elsewhere, PDF exports and Google Docs (which record no editing time) blur it for documents. Don't confront a participant on this alone.
- **Dashboard tab:** a tile per team (expected injects answered, median first reply, graded score, AI-suggested score, sentiment of each reply over the exercise, tone mix, a delivered → read → answered → graded funnel), the **rubric results** heatmap (switch between *Graded* and *AI suggested*), **where the AI and the graders disagree** (a quarter of the points or more: check the rubric or the reply), **Flags**, **Possibly AI-written** (60 % and above), **Documents teams sent** (60 % and above), **What teams are asking for** and **Decisions and commitments**. Once there are team roles (3.2), **By team role** groups the members of all teams by role: members, messages written (in all and per member), their share of the writing, injects answered, median minutes to reply from first seeing what they answer, inject messages read per member, documents opened, inbox actions (star, archive, label, search, send…), SIMEX AI's quality reading, likely AI-written, and the messages written per team (with how many members hold the role there). A team leader's Dashboard has the same table for their team, and shows each writer's team role. **Write the summary** produces a situation summary across teams (themes, each team's strengths and concerns, what ExCon may want to act on now, points for the hotwash) in about a minute.
- **Dashboard → Network** (*Figures | Network* at the top of the Dashboard; *Exercise* or *Test team*): a live map of the mail. Each dot is a team's inbox (in the team's colour), a team leader (a diamond in it: they also stand for the team leader persona they play), a member (a small dot in the team's colour), a persona (grey; a dashed ring: an AI persona) or ExCon (a square, for a message sent as no persona). A dot grows with the mail it sent and received; a line, with the messages one way: each direction is its own line, curving to its side, bright where the mail leaves and faint where it lands, so a thick line out and a thin one back is a team not answering. A new message travels along its line as a glowing dot, the line lights up for a few seconds and the dots ring; the **Latest** list beside the map has it too. It updates every few seconds without reloading. To keep it readable: **Teams** (the default) folds the members into their team; click a team to open it up into its members and their mail with each other and the leader (click again to fold it in), or choose **People** to open every team. **Time** counts only the last 15 minutes, the last hour or today (with *Whole exercise*, older lines fade). Untick **Injects** to see the conversations without the injects' fans to every team; **Lines from** and **Personas from** hide the lines and personas with fewer messages. Point at a dot for its figures, click it to keep them in the panel (sent, received, who it writes with both ways, its latest subjects) and dim everything it isn't connected to, with arrows on its lines. Drag a dot to pin it (double-click or **Unpin** to let it go); scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan, **Fit** to see all of it again (until you zoom or pan it keeps everything in view by itself), **New layout** to start the layout afresh. **Style** changes the look: a **Theme** (*Auto*: light here, dark on the wall screen; *Light*, *Paper*, *Dark*, *Midnight*, *High contrast*, for a projector or a bright room), the size of the **Dots**, the thickness (**Lines**) and **Line strength** of the lines, how much they **Curve** (none: straight), which **Labels** show (as there's room, every dot, teams and leaders only, none) and their **Label size**, the **Moving messages and glows** (untick for a still map) and the **Legend**; **Default style** puts it back. The layout, choices and style are kept in that browser. **Wall screen** opens it full screen (dark unless another theme is chosen), without the menus, with the real and simulation time: leave it open on a screen in the ExCon room. Counted: every delivery (injects, follow-ups, answers from ExCon, the desk or the AI personas, the leaders' messages), every message a participant writes in SIMEX Mail (to personas, the leader persona, teammates, the team or another team) and replies by email; not withdrawn deliveries, the copies for the team leaders, logins or role-play reminders, nor anyone's reading.
- **Each unsent inject** (SIMEX AI, fast model, before the exercise and again whenever the inject changes): reads it as ExCon wrote it, with the list of all injects for context, and reports only concrete problems: attachments it mentions but doesn't have, unfilled placeholders, notes for ExCon left in, names, places or figures that contradict each other, a date the inject contradicts itself on (a weekday that doesn't match its date), a greeting that doesn't fit the recipients, a persona that doesn't fit the content. It knows the exercise compresses time: jumps in scenario time between injects, scenario times that differ from send times, and injects out of scenario-time order are intended and not reported. They show as purple **AI: …** tags on the Injects list and under **Before sending** on the inject page, with **Check again with AI** and **Dismiss**. On the Injects tab, **Check unchecked with AI** reads only the unsent injects that have no review of them as they are now (new, changed since, or failed), **Check all** re-reads every unsent inject (the count next to them says how many have been read as they are now), and **Check with AI** in the bar above the list reads just the ones you tick. A full pass over 100 injects costs about five cents.
- **Settings → AI analysis:** on/off; *analyse every reply as it arrives* (off: only when you click **Analyse now**); include Test team replies (useful in rehearsals); *review unsent injects*; *read the files teams send* (on by default; with automatic analysis off, only the files you click **Analyse document** on) and *read documents with the deep model* (a closer reading of long plans at about 20 times the cost; a 20-page plan costs a few cents with Haiku); *on the persona desk, read each conversation a team is waiting on* (5.10; on by default); *ChatSIMEX on the persona desk* (5.13; on by default); a **daily spending cap** in USD (default 5; a full exercise day costs well under a dollar). The card shows today's calls, spend and the last error.
- **Settings → AI instructions:** what SIMEX AI is told for each kind of reading: the **exercise context** (opens all of them), the **inject check**, **reading each reply**, **reading each document**, **rubric suggestions**, the **exercise summary**, **Draft with AI**, **Persona desk: what needs an answer**, and the **team debrief** and **ExCon** reports (6.1). Open one, edit the text and click **Save instructions**; an edited one is marked *edited* and has **Reset to default**. Changes apply to the next reading (use **Re-run** to redo one); changing the inject check (or the exercise context) checks every unsent inject again. You can change what SIMEX AI looks for and how it writes, not what it is given or the fields it answers with. Never paste control-side material (expected outcomes, story, key figures) here.
- What SIMEX AI sees: the inject as the team received it (for the inject review: as written, with the subjects of the other injects), the rubric you wrote, and the teams' replies (for a draft: all the injects, the personas and what the teams wrote). Nothing else is stored in the app to send.

### 5.8 Draft with AI
SIMEX AI can draft a message for you to edit and send. It never sends anything. Settings → AI analysis must be on; a draft takes a few seconds and costs about a cent (ten cents for the first one after the injects change, which SIMEX AI has to read in full again).
- **Where to start:**
  - **Injects → Draft with AI**: say what you need ("the governor of Plaida complains on the radio that the team ignored the island"), optionally an inject it follows on from and a team it reacts to.
  - **On an inject**, the **Draft with AI** card: *a new inject that follows on from this one* or, once it is delivered, *a follow-up in this conversation*. You can pick a team to react to.
  - **On a reply**: **Draft an answer** (the persona's answer in the same conversation) or **Draft a new inject reacting to this**.
- **What it uses:** every inject (the text of each one, and whether the teams have it yet), the personas, and what the teams wrote. When it starts from a team, it reads that team's whole thread on the inject and a line for each of its recent messages. It writes in the style of the existing injects from that kind of sender, keeps to the places, figures and names the teams have been given, and doesn't give away planned injects. The app holds no storyline, expected outcomes or key figures, so check any **new facts** it lists against the scenario.
- **The draft page** shows the message (persona, subject, scenario time, suggested send time and recipients, whether a reply is expected, files it mentions) and, for ExCon only, **why** (what it does in the exercise), **new facts to check**, **to confirm** and **built on**. **Revise** asks for changes ("shorter, from WFP instead") and keeps the earlier draft. **Recent drafts** lists them all.
- **Using it:**
  - **Make it a draft inject** creates an unscheduled inject `AI-n` with the draft's sender, text, scenario time, suggested send time, recipients (the team only, if the draft is for one team), reply expectation and the documents it mentions. The documents show as missing until you attach them. The AI review then reads it like any other inject. Edit it, then schedule or send it as usual.
  - A follow-up opens the inject's **Send a follow-up** form, filled in and ticked for the team it was drafted for.
  - An answer opens the reply's **Answer as <persona>** box, filled in.
  - Either way you read it and send it yourself. A persona SIMEX AI made up shows as *new persona*: add it on **Senders** or pick an existing one.
- The app records which injects, follow-ups and answers were sent from an AI draft, and whether you changed the text first. The end-of-exercise reports count them (6.1).

### 5.9 AI personas
Some personas can answer the teams on their own, like non-player characters in a game: a deputy governor, an agency contact, a journalist. SIMEX AI writes each answer in character, after a delay drawn from the persona's timing, in the same conversation, in SIMEX Mail or by email. Turn it on in **Settings → AI personas** (off by default; AI analysis must be on too). It answers **the Test team only** until you choose **every team** there. The **AI personas** tab is always there: a persona's profile also gives **Suggest a reply** (5.12) and **ChatSIMEX** (5.13) its voice and knowledge, even while it doesn't answer on its own. So give every persona the desk plays a profile, and switch some to answering on their own only when the desk needs a hand.
- **Making one** (AI personas tab):
  - **From the injects**: a profile for each persona your injects come from. SIMEX AI reads each one's injects to work out who they are and how they write. Their knowledge comes only from what the teams have been told, never from their planned injects. This is one call each, about a cent apiece.
  - **Generate with AI**: describe a new one ("the deputy governor of the worst-hit province: proud, defensive, knows which roads are cut; slow to answer"). SIMEX AI writes the name, address, profile and timing from your brief and the injects.
  - **From an existing persona**: give a persona on **Senders** a profile, written by you or by SIMEX AI from its injects.
  - **Import profiles**: a spreadsheet or CSV, one persona per row by its **Username** (or its SIMEX Mail address), with any of *Organisation*, *Role*, *Personality and voice*, *What they know and may share* (one fact per line), *Boundaries*, *Timing* (a preset, by name or key, or *custom* with *Custom: from / to (min)*), *Office hours from / to*, *Sends straight away* and *Answers on its own* (yes / no). You see the plan first: which persona each row is, a new profile or what changes (*changes*: it replaces a value set here), and the rows skipped (not on **Senders**, the team leader persona, `{team}` personas, a timing or yes/no it can't read). Untick a row to leave that persona as it is, then **Apply**. Empty cells leave a value as it is; names and addresses stay with **Senders**. New profiles stay off unless the file says *Answers on its own: yes* (the plan warns of those). **Export** gives the profiles in the same columns, then a row for each persona without one: fill them in and import it back. What a file puts in a profile reaches the teams through the persona's answers: only what the teams may learn.
  - Profiles SIMEX AI writes are **off**, and new personas are out of the address book, until you have read them and saved them. Check the **new facts** SIMEX AI lists against the scenario. Team leader personas and `{team}` personas can't be AI personas.
- **Switching over** (the list on the AI personas tab): tick personas, then **Answer on their own** (with *and answer the conversations already waiting on the desk for them*, ticked by default: each conversation where a team's latest message to one of them is unanswered and nothing is queued gets an answer within minutes) or **Stop answering** (the desk answers them again; an answer already queued is skipped when it comes due). Settings → AI personas must be on, and set to **every team**, for them to answer the real teams.
- **The profile**:
  - who they are: name, username, the address shown in SIMEX Mail, role, organisation
  - **personality and voice**
  - **what they know and may share**: only what the teams may learn, never control-side material
  - **boundaries**: what they won't share or promise
  - **timing**: *Instant* (1–3 min), *Responsive* (5–20 min), *Busy official* (20–90 min, sometimes within minutes, sometimes never), *Bureaucrat* (1–4 h), *Unpredictable* (often within minutes, else hours, 15 % never), or *Custom*, with optional office hours in exercise time
  - **Answers on its own**; **sends its answers straight away** (untick it to read and send each answer yourself); **in the address book**

  The wait is drawn when a message arrives. More messages in the same conversation before the persona answers get one answer to all of them.
- **Other personas write to it too** (persona mail, 5.10): written to as a persona on the desk or from Compose, an AI persona answers that persona the same way, the teams in copy reading it if any; two AI personas keep a conversation going for at most 3 answers in a row. Handy for trying one out with no team, and for background chatter between partners. Its answers show in its queue as *persona mail*.
- **What it knows when answering**: its profile, the other personas, every inject the teams have received, the conversation, and what it told the same team before. It doesn't invent official figures or events. When asked something outside its knowledge, it answers as a real person would ("I'll check").
- **Try it** (the persona's page): write as a team and see the answer at once, with how long it would have made the team wait. Nothing is delivered.
- **Answers**, on the persona's page and the tab:
  - *queued* (with when it is due): **Answer now**, or skip it
  - *held*: edit it, then **Send**, or **Don't answer**
  - *failed* (after three tries): **Try again**
  - *skipped* (its timing drew no answer, or you skipped it): **Answer anyway**

  Each answer shows what it shared, any flags (*asked for something it doesn't know*, *stepped out of the exercise*, *ExCon should look*, *pushed past its boundaries*) and a note for you. The Notifications panel tells you when one is sent, waiting for you or failed.
- On the **Replies** tab, the team's messages to an AI persona are replies like any other. A persona's answers are marked *AI persona answer*. They are left out of ExCon's own answer times in the reports.
- **Team leaders** get an **AI personas** tab in SIMEX Mail (read-only): every conversation their team has had with an AI persona, both ways, with when the persona's answer is due, and the AI personas their team can write to. They never see another team's conversations, or the profiles.
- **Pause all sending** stops the answers too. They go out when you resume. Answers count toward the daily AI cap.

### 5.10 Persona desk
A shared mailbox in SIMEX Mail for the people playing the personas, so answering the teams doesn't have to go through the Replies tab one message at a time. Give each role-player a login with the role **Persona desk (ExCon)** (3.2); you can open it yourself too (**Team inboxes → Open the persona desk**, or `/inbox/desk/` in SIMEX Mail). Several people can be on it at once: they share one mailbox (read, stars, labels, drafts).
- **Inbox**: every message a team writes to a persona, in SIMEX Mail or by email (once fetched), except to the team leader persona (the leaders play it), automatic answers and team leaders' own replies. **Sent**: every delivery from a persona: injects, follow-ups, answers from the Replies tab, the AI personas' answers and what the desk writes. A conversation is normally one team's; an email inject sent to all teams in Bcc is one conversation, which every team's answers join.
- **Telling the personas apart**: a coloured tag per persona (its colour on the Senders tab) and a grey tag for the team on every conversation and message, a coloured edge on each row and message, and in the side bar a filter per persona and per team with their unread conversations. Each persona message says whether it was an inject, a follow-up, sent by ExCon, by a desk member (by name) or written by an AI persona.
- **Reply** answers as the persona the team wrote to: it is that message's answer, exactly as **Answer as** on the reply's page (the Replies tab shows it, the team's next reply is matched to it), in SIMEX Mail or by email as the conversation is. **From** lists every active persona but the team leader persona: choosing another one writes as that persona in the same conversation. **Write as a persona** starts a new conversation with one or more teams, team members or team leaders: its **From** is empty (red, *Choose the persona it is from…*) until you pick the persona, and nothing is sent without one, so a message never goes out as whichever persona happens to be first (one message each; typing in To, Cc or Bcc suggests every active team, member and leader, by name or address). To send the same message to every team, pick **All teams** (first in the suggestions, or type *All teams*): it puts a chip for each team but the Test team, which you can then remove one by one. Each team gets its own message, as if written to it alone: no team sees the others in To or Cc, and each team's reply is a conversation of its own on the desk. **Other personas** can be put in To or Cc too, with or without teams: **persona mail**. Each team's message shows them (by email, in its To or Cc), and the team's **Reply all** reaches them, coming to the desk as one message to both. The desk's Inbox gets the message for them, in one conversation with the teams' copies (no team waits on it, so it is never in Needs attention, and it isn't in the Dashboard's figures). Written to personas only, no team sees it at all and no live arming is needed: use it to try out an AI persona, or for background chatter between partners, with teams in Cc when they should read it. **Reply** on persona mail answers the persona who wrote, as the persona it went to (**Reply all**: everyone on it, teams too). An **AI persona in To** (not Cc) answers on its own, with its timing, to whoever wrote and everyone else on it (teams in copy only while its scope, Settings → AI personas, covers them all); AI personas answering each other stop after 3 answers in a row in a conversation, until someone on the desk writes in it again. Someone on the desk answering as the AI persona first cancels its answer. One in Bcc is simply left out. The desk's calendar still invites teams, members and leaders only. **Clear mailboxes** for every team and every mailbox clears persona mail too. A team leader's (e.g. **Team A leader (Jane Doe)**) goes to the leader's own mailbox only, never the team's (by email, to the leader's email address). SIMEX Mail doesn't let a leader write to personas, so they can only read it there; by email, their answer comes into the Replies tab as *from the team leader*. A member's goes to their own mailbox only (**My mail**; with personal mailboxes off, to the team inbox, marked for them; by email, to their own email address), and the persona's answers to their reply go on there. Messages the desk writes that aren't answers are logged as kind *desk*: they have no inject, so they are kept out of the injects' delivery and reply figures, but the teams' replies to them are matched like any other. New messages need **live sending armed** (not for the Test team); nothing goes while sending is **paused**.
- **What still needs an answer**: read or unread doesn't tell several people whether a team is still waiting, so the **Inbox** comes in sections. **Needs attention** first: conversations where a team's latest message has no answer from a persona yet (whoever on the desk read it), follow-ups that are due, and AI persona answers held for ExCon or failed; the longest waiting first (with SIMEX AI on, the urgent ones above), each with *Waiting 12 min*. Then **Probably no reply needed (SIMEX AI)**: waiting conversations SIMEX AI reads as only acknowledging or thanking. Then **Later**: follow-ups not due yet and conversations an AI persona is about to answer (*AI persona answers at 14:20*). Then **Answered or nothing to answer**. The side bar says how many need attention; the persona and team filters are in sections too. To see them like any email instead, newest at the top, click **In sections · show newest first** above the list (rows still say *Waiting 12 min* and who has them); **Newest first · show in sections** goes back. Each login's choice is kept as it moves around SIMEX Mail. A conversation stops waiting as soon as any persona writes to that team in it (from the desk, the Replies tab, a follow-up or an AI persona); a new message from the team starts it again.
- **Several conversations at once**: keep the Inbox in one browser tab and open conversations in others. Point at a row and click the arrow-in-a-box at its right (**Open in a new tab**), Ctrl-click (⌘-click on a Mac) or middle-click the row, or press **Shift+O** on the row picked with **j** / **k**. The list stays open in its tab, and coming back to it reloads it, so what was answered in the other tabs has moved on (not while conversations are ticked or a message is being written). This works in every SIMEX Mail list, including the teams' mailboxes read through the Teams tab.
- **On each conversation**, a bar says where it stands and offers: **I'll take it** (the others see your name on its row; **Release**, or **Take it over**), **Assign to…** (any active desk member or ExCon login: they are notified, and whoever had it is told; the bar then says *Assigned to … by …*), **Follow** / **Unfollow** (hear what happens in it even when someone else has it), **No reply needed** (out of Needs attention until the team writes again, then on to the next conversation waiting; **Reopen** undoes it), **Follow up** now or in 15 minutes to 4 hours with a note (in Later until it is due, then in Needs attention, until a persona writes in it; **Stop** ends it), **Ask SIMEX AI** and **Next waiting**. Select rows in the Inbox for **I'll take these**, **Assign to…**, **No reply needed** (also the ✓ next to Delete above the list) and **Reopen** on many at once; a row's ✓ (on hover) is **No reply needed**, and so is the ✓ in an open conversation's toolbar (next to Delete; **Reopen** there once it is marked). Rows also show who on the desk is reading or writing in a conversation now. Each row of the desk's lists has two lines: the subject and the start of the latest message, then where it stands (how long the team has waited, *Urgent*), the persona and the team, who has it or is in it now, and last what SIMEX AI reads the team as asking (cut short first when the row is narrow).
- **Suggest a reply** (under the bar, with AI analysis on): SIMEX AI's suggested answer from the persona, built on what this team has been given so far, with notes on its sources and what not to get ahead of (5.12). **Reply with this** opens the reply with it.
- **ChatSIMEX** (the button at the right of the desk's tabs): ask SIMEX AI anything about the exercise in your own words: *who is Salma?*, *what has been said about the bridge?*, *how do I answer this?* (5.13).
- **SIMEX AI's reading** (Settings → AI analysis, *on the persona desk, read each conversation a team is waiting on*, on by default, with AI analysis on): for each waiting conversation, once per new team message, whether it needs a reply, is optional or needs none, how urgent, and what the team asks in a line (on its row; the reason under it on the conversation). It reads only the conversation itself. With *analyse every reply as it arrives* off, only when someone clicks **Ask SIMEX AI**. It is a hint: it never closes or answers anything.
- **Assigned to me, Following**: at the top of the desk's side bar. **Assigned to me** lists the conversations you took or were given, and its number is how many of them need attention. **Following** lists the ones you are involved in but don't have: you answered in them, set a follow-up or clicked **Follow**. Both come in sections like the Inbox.
- **Your notifications** (the bell at the top of SIMEX Mail on the desk, with the number you haven't read; each person's own, ExCon logins on the desk too, separate from ExCon's Notifications panel). They cover:
  - a conversation given to you, taken over or reassigned from you;
  - your follow-up falling due;
  - a team writing in a conversation you have, or follow (while nobody has it, everyone involved in it hears);
  - someone else (or an AI persona) answering in it;
  - an AI persona's answer to it held or failed;
  - a team waiting **Notify ExCon after** minutes in one you have.

  Click one to open the conversation; **✕**, **Mark all read** and **Clear all** are in the panel. Conversations given to you, follow-ups due and teams waiting too long are also pushed to the desk member's devices with alerts on. A notification for something new while the page is open shows at the bottom of the screen.
- **Your settings on the desk** (the gear at the top, next to the bell; each login's own, ExCon logins on the desk too, kept through **Start afresh**, in backups). **Only my conversations**: notifications, the page's chime and pop-up for new mail, and pushes to their devices only for conversations they have or follow (Assigned to me, Following); being given a conversation and their own follow-ups still come. A new conversation nobody has is then silent for them (it still shows in the Inbox, its count and the Assign board). Saved as it is clicked.
- **Teams waiting too long**: when a team has waited 20 minutes (Settings → Persona desk) for an answer in Needs attention, ExCon's Notifications panel says so, with who took it, once per message waiting; it opens the conversation on the desk. Not for conversations an AI persona is about to answer or SIMEX AI reads as needing no reply.
- **AI personas**: answering a conversation as an AI persona cancels that persona's queued answer to it (the AI personas queue shows *Answered by … on the persona desk*).
- **Dashboard** (tab, `/inbox/desk/dashboard/`, also for ExCon on the desk): how the desk is keeping up, from its own mailbox, refreshed every minute. Tiles for what needs attention now (and how many nobody has taken), the longest a team is waiting, the median time to answer (last hour and overall), the share answered within the notification time (Settings → Persona desk), messages from the teams, follow-ups and AI persona answers queued or held. An hourly strip of the teams' messages against the answers. **Waiting longest**, with who has each. **Requests to the personas**: what the teams asked each persona, unanswered ones first (SIMEX AI's reading of each reply, as on the ExCon Dashboard), and **Decisions and commitments** and **Figures and facts the teams state** to each persona, so follow-ups and answers stay consistent with them. **Teams** (one being left behind shows at the top), **Personas** (which ones carry the load), **Who is answering** (answers, median time, what each has taken now, online or not), **Who has which conversations** (each desk member, and any ExCon login with one: how many they have, need attention, the longest wait, follow-ups due and later, answered, and their conversations under their name; the number opens them as a list; **Nobody has them**: the open ones nobody has) and **Flags for the desk** (needs ExCon attention, safety, wrong persona, out of the exercise). *Exercise teams* / *Test team* switches between the two. A team message counts as answered by the first persona message to that team after it in the conversation; injects and follow-ups that came next count as answered, but not in the times. It never shows grades, quality scores, tone or the AI-written estimate.
- **Assign** (tab, `/inbox/desk/assign/`, also for ExCon on the desk) has two views. **Board**: the open conversations as cards in columns, **Not assigned** first and then each desk member (and, with *ExCon logins*, ExCon staff; anyone with a conversation always shows), with how many each has, need attention and the longest wait, whether they are online, and the personas they have by default. Drag a card onto someone to give it to them, or onto **Not assigned** to release it, exactly as **Assign to…** (they are notified; several cards moved at once give one notification). Click cards to select several; on a touch screen drag by the dots at a card's top left, or use the menu on each card. Filters: team, persona, *and answered, last 1 / 4 / 24 h*, Exercise teams / Test team. It updates itself every few seconds. **Default assignments**: a grid of personas (rows; not the team leader persona) by teams (columns), each cell the desk member or ExCon login who has that persona's conversations with that team; **All teams** covers the teams without their own cell (e.g. CCPRR: All teams to one person, Team B and Team D to another). **Whole row to…** and **Clear the row**; every change is saved at once and audited. A conversation is given to its default person once, when it starts: at the persona's first delivery to the team (quietly: they hear when the team writes, as anyone who has it) or the team's first message to the persona; persona mail (personas writing to each other, no team) by the **All teams** cell of the persona written to, else of the writer. Never over a choice made by hand: a conversation anyone took, assigned or released keeps it; it shows *by default* on its card and *by the default assignments* on its bar. **Apply to open conversations nobody has** gives those to their default person now. Someone who is no longer an active desk login is skipped. Default assignments are setup like the Senders: kept through **Start afresh** and **Clear mailboxes**, in backups, gone with their persona.
- **Who else is on it**: a conversation shows who else on the desk has it open, and who is writing an answer.
- **Network** (tab, `/inbox/desk/network/`, also for ExCon on the desk): the same live map of who writes to whom as **Dashboard → Network** (5.7), with *Exercise teams* / *Test team* and its own **Wall screen**; the same controls, kept in that browser. It shows who wrote to whom and the subjects, as the desk can already read in every team's mailboxes.
- **Injects** (tab, `/inbox/desk/injects/`, read-only, also for ExCon on the desk; Settings → Persona desk → *show the injects to the persona desk*, on by default): every inject by send time, grouped by day, the next one highlighted with how long until it goes; each with its status, persona, subject and the start of its text, who gets it (teams, per team or per participant, and the team leaders) and how (SIMEX Mail or email, files, reply expected). *Coming up* (scheduled, overdue, sending or failed), *Sent*, *All* (also those not scheduled and cancelled); filters by persona, team and words. An inject's page shows when it goes (and its scenario time), from whom, to whom, its files (to download) and its text as each team gets it (placeholders filled; one block per distinct version), and where it has gone so far, each delivery opening its conversation on the desk; a delivered inject in a desk conversation links back to it. Never ExCon's checks, SIMEX AI's review, errors, rubrics or grades, nor the recipients' email addresses. Turn it off if the people on the desk shouldn't see what is coming.
- **Filtering the list**: besides **Unread**, **Starred**, **Has files** and **Not replied** (section 1), the desk's lists have one-click **Needs attention**, **Urgent** (SIMEX AI's reading), **Mine**, **Nobody has it** and **Waiting 30 min+**, and **More filters**: where a conversation stands (needs attention, a team waiting, urgent, probably no reply needed, follow-up set, AI persona answering, marked no reply needed; any of those ticked), one or more personas and teams, who has it (me, nobody, someone, or a named desk member or ExCon login), a team waiting longer than 15 minutes to 2 hours, latest message in the last 15 minutes to 12 hours, and live or test traffic. The filters in use show as tags (**×** removes one; **Clear filters** all). They work in every desk folder and in the sections order, are kept in the address (bookmark a filtered list) and through search, paging and the arrows on a conversation, and are never stored or recorded.
- **Teams & team leaders** (tab): every team's inbox, its leader's mailbox and members' own mailboxes, read-only, as **View as team**; nothing is marked read for them. The desk never sees rubrics, grades or SIMEX AI's reading of the replies (only its own reading of what needs an answer, and the requests, commitments, figures and desk flags on its Dashboard, above).
- Desk members turn on alerts like participants: each new message to a persona is pushed to their devices. Their guide is under **?** in SIMEX Mail.
- Reading on the desk is never counted as the team reading. **Clear mailboxes** (every mailbox) also clears the desk's copies of that team's mail, with who had and followed those conversations and the notifications about them; **Start afresh** clears all of it.

### 5.11 Role play
The people who play characters face to face, by phone or by VTC (airport staff, immigration, the RC, the press, the team leaders' scripted calls) need to be at the right place at the right time, for the right team, with their script. The **Role play** tab is ExCon's board for that. Each role player has the same on their phone, with reminders.

**Control-side, kept apart.** Scripts, expected outcomes and notes are kept here, unlike for injects. They are shown only on this tab and to the role players assigned to each role play, and read by **Suggest a reply** (5.12) for its notes to the persona desk and ExCon. Teams, team leaders' mailboxes, the rest of the AI (analysis, AI personas, drafts) and the reports never see them.

**Load the schedule: Role play → Import.** Upload the MEL-MIL workbook and the role-player schedule sheet, in one workbook or one after the other:
- From the **MEL-MIL** sheet, its rows played face to face, by phone or VTC (Inject method *F2F*, *Phone*, *VTC*, *MS Teams*, *Signal*): serial, day, time, duration, title, **script** (Content), **expected outcomes** and documents. The email rows stay with the injects.
- From the **role-play scripts document** (the role players' scripts in Word, `.docx`, or saved as `.md` / `.txt`: for each event a bold line with the serial and title, *1-45   Oxfam visit the OSOCC*, then the day, the time(s) in bold, the documents in italics and the script): each role play's **script**, replacing the MEL's. An event that isn't here yet also gets its title, day, time and documents; one already here keeps everything else, and nothing is deleted for not being in the document.
- From the **role-player schedule** (columns *Serial, Time, Duration, Role, Role player, Event*): the role and who plays it. Names are matched to People (role players, team leaders, the persona desk); new names are added as role players without a login. A serial on two rows (the RC with Peter + Janet for teams A&C, with Ben + Aida for B&D) makes two role plays, sharing the MEL's script. *n/a* leaves it unassigned.
- Times become **sittings**, one per team where the sheet says so:
  - `1440 - A`, or one line per team (`1430 - A`, `1450 - B`…)
  - `18:00 A - B / 18:20 C - D`
  - `18:00 + 18:20` with the role *RC - teams A&C* (A at 18:00, C at 18:20)
  - `19:10` for `4 x 5 min (D C B A)` (D at 19:10, C at 19:15…)
  - `4 x 5 min` with no order: the teams in order

  A plain time is one sitting for every team together.
- The sheets name the day only (*Monday*). The plan shows the date each day stands for, guessed from the MEL's email rows: correct them there, or later under **Role players & briefing → Exercise days**.
- The plan lists what is new, updated and edited by you (kept unless you tick *overwrite my edits*). The checkboxes at the top choose the sheets: an old schedule kept in the workbook (other serials) starts unticked. A re-import never moves a sitting that has started.

**Role players: Role play → Role players & briefing.**
- Add role players there or on **People & logins** (role **Role player (ExCon)**, no team). Give them a login and email it or print the slip as for anyone (3.2). The QR code signs them in on their phone, opens their role plays and turns on alerts (on an iPhone, after adding it to the Home Screen).
- Write the **briefing** and **FAQ** every role player sees, and set the reminders. They come so many minutes before each sitting (15 by default) and again at its start, unless the role player is already in position. Optionally they're emailed to role players with no alerts on any device.
- The list shows each role player's login, alert devices, when they last opened their schedule, their next sitting, and a link to their **run sheet** (printable, with their scripts).
- A **team leader** can be given role plays too (*Team Leaders: Silva - Stefania…*). Each leader plays only their own team's sittings (or those for every team), and gets a **My role plays** tab in SIMEX Mail.

**During the exercise: Role play → Board.**
- Every sitting of the day by time: the role play, the team, the role and who plays it (🔔 = alerts on), where, and its status. A role player taps **I'm in position** and **Done** on their phone; you can set either here too, or **skip** a sitting. The board refreshes itself.
- A sitting past its start with nobody in position is marked *late*, and a notification says so (Notifications → System).
- **Slip the schedule** moves every *pending* sitting from a time to the end of that day by so many minutes (negative: earlier). Done and in-position sittings stay. The role players affected get *Changed: 1-29 now 15:25 (was 15:10)*.
- **Checks**:
  - a role player double-booked
  - sittings with no time
  - role plays with no role player, or face to face with no location
  - a team in two places at once (fine if it splits up)
- **Persona(s) this character is** (on a role play's page, optional): the usernames of the personas this character is, when the role play plays a persona the teams also write to (*dr-amani, moh-press*). The page also lists the personas found by name (their name in the role, title or script), so you only need to fill it in when the name doesn't say. **Suggest a reply** and **ChatSIMEX** then give the persona's answers that character's voice (5.12, 5.13). Nobody needs to be assigned to play it, and whoever answers the persona's mail on the desk gets the same voice.
- Open a role play to edit anything, assign role players, add, move or delete sittings (moving one tells its role players) or **Rebuild from the time text**, and upload files (handouts, signs). Role players download the files from their phone.
- **Print run sheets**: the day's sittings with every script, or `?player=` / `?location=` for one role player or one place, with or without the scripts.

**What a role player sees** (`/inbox/rp/` in SIMEX Mail):
- **Now** and **Next**, with a countdown
- their whole schedule by day, each sitting with its team, place, role and who else plays it
- for each role play, the script, what the teams should do, documents, files and notes
- the **Briefing & FAQ**

Everything on it is marked as Exercise Control's, never to be shown to a team.

### 5.12 Suggest a reply and the Library
With many messages coming in, SIMEX AI can suggest a persona's answer to a team's message, looking things up first the way a facilitator would in the exercise package. It never sends anything. **Suggest a reply** is on every persona desk conversation a team has written in (under the desk bar) and on a reply's page (Replies tab, above **Answer as**). AI analysis must be on.
- **Ask**: optionally say what the answer should do (*keep it short*, *say no politely*), then **Suggest a reply**. It takes about half a minute to a minute, while SIMEX AI searches and reads, and costs a few cents. You can leave the page; the latest suggestion for the conversation shows when you come back, and anyone else on the desk sees it too.
- **What SIMEX AI reads**, each item marked with where *this team* stands on it now:
  - the conversation, the team's other recent messages and the persona (its Senders notes, its AI persona profile if it has one);
  - **the desk's other mail** (what the personas and the teams wrote to each other, never a team's internal mail): this persona's other conversations with this team, the other personas' recent exchanges with it (the state of play), and **what this persona told the other teams** and what they asked it, so one persona tells every team the same facts and positions. SIMEX AI can search all of it too. What another team asked, said or was told just for it is never passed on: a fact the answer takes from another team's exchange is flagged under **Check before sending**;
  - the **Library** (below): the participants' documents, each *the team has it*, *not yet* (it comes with an inject or role play the team hasn't had) or *not given*;
  - the **injects**: delivered to this team, or still to come for it (with their scenario and send times);
  - the **role plays** (Role play tab): their scripts, expected outcomes and notes, and whether each has been played with this team yet.
  - **the persona's character in the role plays**: when the persona is played face to face, by phone or VTC too (the role plays linked to it on their page, or naming it), the scripts' passages on that character. The answer is written in that character's voice, temperament and attitude, so the team meets the same person in the mail and in the room, whether the sitting has happened yet or not and whoever plays it or answers the mail; facts still come only from the sittings the team has had.
- **The timeline is the point.** The answer may use only what the team has been given or told so far: the documents it has, the injects it received, the role plays it has had, the conversation. What is still to come is for your notes only, never the answer, so the team learns nothing early. When the team asks for something the package doesn't have (GPS coordinates, a figure nobody gave), the persona says they don't have it; SIMEX AI doesn't invent official figures or coordinates.
- **What you get**: the answer, ready to send (**Reply with this** opens the reply with it on the desk; **Put it in the answer** on the reply's page; **Copy**), and for you: **Check before sending** (any fact the answer rests on that this team doesn't have yet, or that SIMEX AI made up), what the team asks, **Corrections** (what the team got wrong), **Not in the package**, **Don't get ahead of** (what is still to come for this team and when, e.g. a later call from the same organisation), notes, **To decide**, and **What the answer rests on** (each fact with its source and whether the team has it). Read it, edit it, send it as usual. Using the answer (**Reply with this** or **Put it in the answer**) puts the suggestion away, and **×** dismisses it; either way it stays hidden in your browser (**Show the last suggestion** brings it back) until you ask for a new one.
- **Who sees it**: the persona desk and ExCon only, as the notes can quote role-play scripts and planned injects. Participants, team leaders, the AI personas and the reports never see suggestions.
- **For team leaders** (Settings → AI analysis, *team leaders can ask for a suggested reply*, on by default while AI analysis is on): in their own mailbox, above a conversation in which a member or the team wrote to them, the same panel drafts their answer as the team leader persona. It is built from what the leader may see only: the conversation, the team's mail (the team inbox and their own mailbox), the injects delivered to the team, the Library documents the team has (released to all, or with an inject it has had or a role-play sitting it has had) and background guidance. It never reads what is still to come or not given, role plays, ExCon's notes on personas, AI analyses or grades, so it gives only the answer, what the message asks, questions for the leader and each fact's source (a warning if one isn't something the team has). **Reply with this** opens the leader's reply with it; nothing is sent for them. Each request is a few deep-model calls (AiCall *suggest_tl*), under the daily cap.
- **The Library** (Injects → Library, ExCon): the documents the participants have or may be given (baseline documents, inject documents, maps), as text, from the knowledge base. In `undac-simex`, run `python3 -I scripts/export_ops.py` (add `--list` to see what goes in and what is left out) and upload `work/ops-library.json.gz` here. It holds only participant-facing documents: never the MEL, expected outcomes, role-player scripts or management files. Baseline documents are marked *Participants have it*; a document whose name starts with a serial (*3-14 MoH health facility status.pdf*) *Released with* that inject or role play, so a team has it once that inject reached it or that role play was played with it (a file attached to an inject counts as given with it, whatever its setting); the rest (the MapAction maps, for instance) *Not given* until you say otherwise. Correct any document's setting on its row; uploading again keeps your settings and updates the text.
- **Background or supporting documents** (Library → *Add background or supporting documents*): add your own files, such as guidance notes, handbooks and standards (the WHO EMT coordination handbook, the note on managing non-classified teams...), or an exercise document the export lacks. PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, text and Markdown are read for their text (several files at once; a scanned PDF without a text layer can't be searched). Choose their setting as you upload: **Background guidance** (the default) tells SIMEX AI the document is published guidance that every team may be assumed to know, to be used for how things are done (procedures, standards, roles, terms) so the persona's answer gets them right, and never as a source of scenario facts; or any of the settings above for an exercise document. A category (default *guidance*) and, for a single file, a title can be given; rename a document on its page. Uploading a file with the same name again replaces its text; uploading the knowledge base's export never removes these files (remove one on its page).

### 5.13 ChatSIMEX: ask SIMEX AI on the persona desk
A chat for the people on the persona desk, so they can brief themselves before they answer: what has already been said about something, what is still to come, who a persona or a name is, and how to answer a team. **ChatSIMEX**, at the right of the desk's tabs, opens it beside the mail on every desk page (the Teams' mailboxes too), for desk members and for ExCon logins on the desk; it stays open as you move around. AI analysis and Settings → AI analysis, *ChatSIMEX on the persona desk* (on by default) must be on. It never sends anything.
- **Ask** in your own words (Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line). An answer takes about half a minute to a minute while SIMEX AI looks things up (you see what it is searching), and costs a few cents (AiCall *chat*, under the daily cap). Ask follow-up questions in the same chat: it remembers the last six questions and answers. **New chat** starts afresh; **History** lists your earlier chats (**✕** deletes one).
- **Your chats are yours alone.** Nobody else on the desk, and no ExCon login, can open them. **Start afresh** clears every chat.
- **Scope**: on a desk conversation, the chat is about that conversation (its team and persona; the chip at the top, **✕** to ask about everything). Choose a team, or **All teams** (the Test team left out unless chosen). Click a persona's tag in an answer, or **Ask about them** under a briefing, to keep asking about that persona.
- **What SIMEX AI reads**:
  - the **desk's mailbox**: everything the personas and the teams wrote to each other;
  - the **teams' own mail**: each team's inbox, its leader's mailbox and (with personal mailboxes on) its members' own, exactly what the desk can open under **Teams & team leaders**. Nothing is marked read and nothing is counted as the team reading;
  - the **Library**, the **injects** and the **role plays** (scripts, expected outcomes, notes), as Suggest a reply does, each marked with where each team stands on it: *has it*, *NOT YET* or *not given*;
  - the **personas**: their Senders notes and AI persona profile, their character in the role plays (as for Suggest a reply: the scripts' passages on them, for how to play them and a draft in their voice, whoever plays it), and the list of every persona;
  - the **roster** (People & logins): who is actually playing in each team, its team leader, the members' team roles and SIMEX Mail addresses, whether each is active and when last seen, and the team's desk conversations (never their email addresses). *Who is on Team A?* is answered from it, not from the team lists in the exercise documents, which describe the scenario's teams.
- **Who is …?** gives a briefing card: who they are (organisation, role, whether they are an AI persona), how to play them, what they know, what they have said so far to each team, what is coming (*NOT YET*: hold it back), and conversations open with them now and who has them. Names are matched loosely (a first name, a surname, a spelling). If the name is no persona, it says what it is: a character in a role play, a name in a document, a participant; or that nothing was found.
- **How do I answer …?** says what the team asks, what the persona can and can't say now, what the persona told other teams about it, and what to hold back; with a **draft** when you ask for one or a conversation is pinned. **Use in reply** opens the reply with it (on its own conversation), or **Copy**. **Check before sending** warns when the draft rests on something that team doesn't have yet, on another team's mail (teams never cross: use other teams' mail to keep the persona consistent, never to pass it on) or on a team's own internal mail (the persona can't know what a team said among itself).
- **References** in an answer: *thread:* opens the desk conversation, *persona:* asks about that persona; *mail:*, *inject:*, *doc:* and *rp:* name the message, inject, Library document or role play it used.
- **Who sees it**: the chat's owner only, on the desk. The answers can quote role-play scripts, planned injects, ExCon's notes on personas and the teams' internal mail, so participants, team leaders, the AI personas and the reports never see them.

### 5.14 SIMEX Calendar and the app drawer
Teams arrange meetings by email all through the exercise. **SIMEX Calendar** is a calendar app in SIMEX Mail where they can put those meetings. Everyone with a SIMEX Mail login has it: members, team leaders and the persona desk. It opens from the **Calendar** tab (the persona desk's, the team leaders' and, with personal mailboxes, the members' tab bars) and from the **app drawer**, the grid of nine dots at the top right of SIMEX Mail, next to **?**. The drawer lists the apps of that login:
- a member: Mail (and My mail), Calendar and Help;
- a team leader: My mail, the team inbox, Team members, Calendar, the Dashboard, Role plays (if any) and Help;
- the persona desk: the desk, Calendar, Teams, the Dashboard, Assign and Help;
- a role player: Role plays and the briefing.

Viewing a team from ExCon, the drawer also has that mailbox's mail and calendar, and **Back to ExCon**. A red number on a tile is unread mail. On a phone the drawer opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- **Whose calendar.** A calendar belongs to a mailbox, as mail does, so teams never see each other's:
  - a member sees their team's calendar, shared by everyone in the team like the team inbox, and their own (**My calendar**, with personal mailboxes on), and can add to both;
  - a team leader adds to their own calendar, and sees the team's calendar and each member's own read-only (ticked on the left to show them);
  - the persona desk has one calendar per persona, in the persona's colour, and adds events as any of them;
  - ExCon (and the desk) viewing a team (**View as team**, or `/inbox/as/<team>/calendar/`) see all of that team's calendars, read-only.

  Another team leader's mailbox (**Team leaders** tab) has no calendar.
- **Views**: **Day**, **Week** (the default on a computer), **Month** and **Schedule** (a list of the next 30 days, the default on a phone), with **Today**, the arrows, a small month on the left to jump to a date, and a red line for now. Keys: `t` today, `n` / `p` next / previous, `d` `w` `m` `a` the views, `c` create.
- **Adding an event**: **Create**, or drag across the hours on the Day or Week grid (click once for an hour; in Month, click a day). Give it a title, when it starts and ends (or **All day**), how (in person, phone, VTC, other), where, a description, and which of your calendars it goes on: **Calendar** starts empty (*Choose a calendar…*), so nothing goes on the first one by mistake, unless you have only one calendar, you drew it on the grid with only one of your calendars shown, or you started from an email (the calendar of the mailbox it is in; on the desk, the persona of the conversation); saving without one says so. Click an event to see it; **Edit** or **Delete** your own. Drag your own events to move them (onto another day too) and their bottom edge to make them longer or shorter; in Month, drag them to another day.
- **Times.** Events are kept in real time. With the simulation clock on (3.4), **members see and enter the scenario's date and time only**, as everywhere in SIMEX Mail. Leaders, the desk and ExCon see the real time, with the scenario time beside it (◷). If you change the simulation clock, events keep their real time and their scenario time moves with the clock. With the clock stopped (speed 0), events are placed as if it ran at 1× from where it stopped.
- **Guests.** Add guests to an event as in Compose: start typing a name from the address book, or type someone's exact address. A calendar can invite whoever its mailbox can write to, checked when the event is saved:
  - from a team's or a member's calendar: the personas, teammates, their own team, and other teams only with team-to-team messages on (Settings);
  - from a team leader's: their team and its members;
  - from the persona desk's: any team, member or team leader.
- **Invitations are mail.** Each guest gets an invitation from the calendar's mailbox, in its usual way of sending, so it is in that mailbox's Sent, pushed like any mail, on the persona desk and the Network. A team's invitation to a persona lands in the persona desk's Inbox as a team message waiting for an answer (it is never matched to an inject); an invitation to the team leader persona reaches that team's leader. In SIMEX Mail an invitation has a card above its text: the event (in the reader's time: the scenario's for members, both for the others), who is invited and their answers, and **Yes**, **Maybe**, **No** with an optional note. The mail itself gives the scenario's time when the simulation clock is on, so members never see a real date in it. In the lists it has an **Invitation** tag. Guests see the event on their own calendar (a persona's on its desk calendar), outlined until they answer, and answer there too.
- **Answers.** An answer goes back to the organiser as a short mail in the same conversation (*Accepted: …*, *Maybe: …*, *Declined: …*), and shows on everyone's card and calendar. On the persona desk, answer as the persona invited: it is that message's answer, and the conversation stops waiting. A team's answer to a persona's invitation needs no reply from the desk: it is marked so on its own. A team leader can't write to personas in SIMEX Mail, so a leader's answer to a persona's invitation is only on the calendar. **AI personas never answer invitations**: the desk always does.
- **Changes.** Only the organiser's calendar can change an event. New guests get an invitation, the others an **Updated invitation** when something changed; if the time or the place changed, they are asked to answer again. A guest taken off, or the whole event deleted, gets a **Cancelled** mail; a cancelled event stays on the guests' calendars, struck through. An event with guests stays on the calendar it was made on.
- **From a conversation.** **Create event** (the calendar icon on a conversation's toolbar) opens a new event with the subject as its title and the people in the conversation as guests. The event links to the email, and the conversation shows *On the calendar: …*.
- **Clashes.** While you choose a time, the editor lists what else is at that time on the calendars you see. There is no free/busy across teams.
- **Personas on the desk.** The persona desk's calendar has one calendar per persona: it organises meetings as any persona and answers the teams' invitations as the persona invited. Guests show with the organisation they speak for. **Who plays it**: a persona's meeting is a conversation on the desk, so whoever has that conversation plays the persona at that time. The event (on the desk's calendar) and its invitation card (on the desk) say who it is, or that nobody has it; **Take it** and **Give to…** there do what they do on the conversation and the **Assign** board, and default assignments apply as usual. The desk calendar's **Show** filter: all the personas' meetings, those you play, those nobody has. The teams never see who plays a persona. The desk hears (its bell) when a team invites a persona, changes or cancels a meeting with one. **AI personas never answer invitations**: they stay with the desk, and the desk's answer cancels any answer an AI persona had queued for that conversation.
- **Compose → Invite to a meeting** (ExCon): a persona, a title, when, how, where, and the teams, members or team leaders invited: the meeting goes on that persona's calendar and the invitations go from the persona (*Sent by ExCon*), exactly as from the desk's calendar.
- **The Network** (5.7) counts an invitation once per message, and an answer as a message back.
- **By email** (Settings → Delivery channel: email). The teams then use their own email, not SIMEX Mail, so they invite nobody from SIMEX Calendar. The persona desk's and Compose's invitations to them go by email, like any desk message, and carry the meeting as a calendar invitation (an `.ics`, *text/calendar*). Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail show it with Accept / Tentative / Decline. A change goes as an updated invitation, and a cancellation removes it from their calendar. Each change goes once to each recipient, never twice. When a guest answers with their mail program, the answer comes back with the email: it is set on the meeting (that guest's *yes / maybe / no* on the desk's calendar, Meetings and the card), and nothing is sent back. The email is still on the Replies tab and the desk like any team message, never counted as an answer to an inject. **Time:** the invitation's calendar part is always in real time (the meeting goes into their own calendar at its real time). With the simulation clock on, the text of the email also gives the scenario's time. The "members never see a real date" rule is for SIMEX Mail only.
- **Withdrawing** a delivery (5.3) that was an invitation takes those teams off the event; an event left without guests is cancelled.
- **Meetings** (ExCon: **Team inboxes → Meetings**, `/meetings/`): every meeting on SIMEX Calendar, across the teams and the personas. Each row has:
  - when (real, with the scenario's time), the title, how and where, and whether it is cancelled;
  - who organised it, and each guest with the organisation they speak for and their answer;
  - for a persona's meeting, who on the desk plays it (or *nobody*), a link to its desk conversation, and its role play;
  - where it came from (*from an email*, *from the mail* for those SIMEX AI found), with links to the conversation and each team's calendar (read-only).

  Filters: exercise teams or the Test team, a team, a persona, on or cancelled, today, the next hours or between dates, and **needs a player** (a persona's meeting nobody on the desk has). **CSV** and **XLSX** export what is shown. The page refreshes itself when anything changes.
- **Make a role play** (ExCon, on Meetings or on the persona desk's calendar): a persona's meeting goes on the **Role play** board (5.11) as `CAL-<number>`, with its title, method (F2F, Phone, VTC), place and length, and one sitting for its teams at its time. Give it role players there as usual. Moving the meeting moves the sitting (its players are told, as after a slip); cancelling it skips the sitting; deleting the role play leaves the meeting. The desk and ExCon see *Role play CAL-…* on the meeting; the teams never do. No script or notes go into it from the calendar, and the calendar never reads them.
- **In the reports** (6.1): each team's report has a line on its meetings: how many it organised, the invitations it received and answered (with the median minutes to answer, from the invitation arriving), those left unanswered or declined, and its meetings with the scenario's organisations. It names no member. ExCon's report has the same for every team in a table.
- **Meetings in the mail** (ExCon: **Team inboxes → Meetings → Find meetings in the mail**, `/meetings/find/`). The calendar came part way through the exercise, so the meetings the teams already arranged by email are only in the mail. Choose teams (none: all) and optionally dates, and click **Find meetings**. SIMEX AI reads the teams' mail only: the team inboxes, leaders' and members' own mailboxes, the persona desk's. It never reads role plays, injects not yet sent, the Library or your notes. It needs AI analysis on, and you get a notification when it is done. It lists each meeting it finds:
  - its time (real, with the scenario time under it; "tomorrow at 10" is read against the date of the message it is in);
  - place, how, organiser and guests (only those in that conversation, so it can never put a team's meeting on another team's calendar);
  - proposed, confirmed, declined or cancelled, and each guest's answer;
  - how sure it is, the quoted lines it rests on (linked to the conversation, read-only), and a warning when it looks like an event already on a calendar (same conversation, or the same people within 30 minutes).

  Correct anything, tick the ones to keep (the sure ones with a time are ticked already) and **Apply the ticked ones**. Each becomes an event on its organiser's and guests' calendars, with the answers the mail shows (a confirmed meeting: everyone accepted). **No mail and no notification goes to anyone.** These events show *From the mail* with a link to the conversation, and the conversation shows *On the calendar (from the mail)*. Running it again skips the conversations already read, unless you tick **Also read conversations earlier runs read**. **Clear mailboxes** removes a team's unapplied meetings; **Start afresh** removes every run; backups keep them.
- **Reminders** (Settings → Calendar → **Remind before an event**, 10 minutes by default, in real minutes; 0 turns them off). When an event is that close, everyone whose calendar it is on gets one notification on their devices with alerts on, e.g. *In 10 min: Coordination meeting · 11:00 · OSOCC tent*:
  - a team's calendar: every member of the team;
  - a member's own: that member;
  - a team leader's own: the leader only;
  - a persona's: on the persona desk, whoever has the conversation or follows it (everyone on the desk while nobody has it), as a desk notification (the bell), pushed.

  Members are given the scenario's time, the others the real time with the scenario's beside it. A guest who declined, and a cancelled event, get none. Each calendar gets one reminder per event, again if the event is moved. One found more than 5 minutes late (the scheduler was down) is dropped, so a restart never sends a burst. With SIMEX Mail open, the reminder also shows at the bottom of the page with the alert sound, once per device, and the app drawer's Calendar tile counts invitations to answer plus events about to start.
- **Upcoming:** the next three events of your calendars, at the bottom of SIMEX Mail's side bar (dashed: not answered yet). Fold it with its arrow; each browser keeps the choice.
- **A persona's meeting with nobody on the desk.** When a persona's accepted meeting starts within the reminder time and nobody on the persona desk has its conversation, ExCon's Notifications panel says so, once per meeting. That is the cue to give it to someone (Assign) to play the persona at that time.
- What members do on their calendars (adding, changing, deleting events) shows in each person's activity (People & logins). It isn't counted as inbox management in the reports or on the Dashboard. **Clear mailboxes** also clears those mailboxes' calendars, **Start afresh** clears every calendar, and backups keep them.

## 6. After the exercise
In this order:
1. **Disarm** and wait for the last answers; **Download backup**.
2. **Finish grading** every team on every inject with a rubric (the Replies grid has no empty cells left for injects you mean to grade). Check *guess* and *unmatched* replies: a reply on the wrong inject counts as a miss in the reports.
3. **Compile the reports** (6.1). Read and edit each team's report; ask the team's leader to read it too (they know the team best). Then **Write it again** on the ExCon report so it reads your edited text.
4. **Print** each team's report to PDF and hand it to that team at the debrief; the ExCon report goes to ExCon and the exercise director.
5. **Export grades (CSV)** and a final backup, then disable the participant logins (People & logins) once the debrief no longer needs them.

### 6.1 End-of-exercise reports
**Dashboard → End-of-exercise reports** (Exercise or Test team). **Compile** makes a report for each team, written for the team to read at the debrief, and one for ExCon comparing the teams. Each compile is a new version; older ones stay listed. Finish grading first: the reports use the grades as they are when you compile.

- **Figures** (from the app's records, also with AI analysis off): injects received and opened; expected replies sent, **missed** and **late**; time to reply (median, 90th percentile, fastest, slowest, from delivery and from the team first opening it, first half of the exercise against the second); **critical points missed** (3.5); every inject with its reply and score; **what the team asked for**, to whom, and whether and how fast the persona answered; writing (clarity, completeness, actionability, words per message, tone); who they corresponded with; how many of the messages they received ExCon drafted with AI; and **how they managed the inbox**: who in the team read and wrote how much, with their team role (numbered, not named, in the team's copy; named on ExCon's) and, with team roles, the same grouped **by team role** (in the ExCon report across all teams too), injects answered before their documents were opened (in-app), mail read but never answered or answered long after, forwards, copies and mail to the team leader, how the inbox was left (unread, archived, starred, unsent drafts), labels, stars later removed, searches, filters used and time spent drafting. The ExCon report adds a table of the teams (and one of their inbox habits), how each inject went across teams, ExCon's own answers (how many, how fast, per persona), AI drafts made, used and edited, failed deliveries and bounces, AI cost, and where the AI-suggested scores and the grades are far apart.
- **The write-up** (SIMEX AI, deep model, AI analysis on; a few minutes; roughly a dollar per team): for each team an overview, timeliness, what went well, what to improve, critical points missed with evidence, a comment on what they asked for, **writing style** with a strong and a weaker quote from their own messages and tips, **how they managed their inbox** (as observations, without singling anyone out; it counts the messages members sent each other from their own mailboxes, not what they said), recommendations and questions for the debrief. It reads only that team's mail: the injects it received, its messages, your answers and follow-ups, the reading of each message and your grades and grader's notes. It uses the human grades only, never the AI-suggested scores. ExCon's report reads the team reports (as you edited them) and a note on every message, and covers the comparison, themes, injects that worked and injects to revise for the next SIMEX, how ExCon ran it, and the AI suggestions against the grades.
- **Inbox habits are recorded from SIMEX Mail only**, from the moment this version was deployed: stars, archive, labels, searches, filters, drafts and sends are logged with their time, besides reads and file downloads. Not ExCon's "view as team", nor a leader reading the team inbox. The participant guide tells participants it is recorded for the debrief. With the email channel only reply times, opens and clicks are known.
- **AI-written estimate** (replies and documents): left out of the team reports (and of what SIMEX AI reads for them) unless you tick **Include the AI-written estimate** when compiling. ExCon always sees it on the report page, marked *ExCon only*.
- **Check and edit:** open each report. Every section has **Edit** (Markdown: `**bold**`, `- ` list items, `> ` quotes) and **Reset to SIMEX AI's**; edited sections are marked. **Write it again** redoes one report from the same figures and replaces your edits to it (write ExCon's again after editing the team reports, so it reads your text). If a write-up fails, the figures are still there.
- **Print:** **Print** on a report, or on the compile **Print the team reports** / **Print all**, opens a printable page: use the browser's Print → Save as PDF. Each report starts on a new page. Give each team only its own report; the ExCon report is not for participants.

### 6.2 Clearing mailboxes and starting afresh
**One team, or all teams, after a test: Team inboxes → Clear mailboxes.** Pick the team (or **All teams**) and the mailboxes, type `CLEAR`:
- **Every mailbox** deletes everything in the team inbox, the team leader's mailbox and each member's own (received, sent, drafts, labels, and what was done in them), the team's replies on the Replies tab with their AI analysis, the readings of the documents it sent, the persona answers to them, the team's grades, the persona desk's copies of its mail and its calendars (5.14).
- **Members' own mailboxes only** empties just the members' My mail and their own calendars; the team inbox, its calendar and its replies stay.
- Deliveries stay in the Log, and in the Dashboard's Delivered and Read, unless you tick **Also delete the deliveries to them**.

**Before going live: Settings → Start afresh.** Type `START AFRESH`. For every team it deletes every mailbox (team inboxes, leaders', members' own, the persona desk) and every calendar, replies, persona answers, grades, AI analyses (documents' too), the exercise summary and reports, test runs, notifications, role-play progress (every sitting pending again at its imported time, slips undone) and every delivery in the Log with its read and download figures: the Dashboard, Replies and Log start from zero. Injects, documents, rubrics, personas, people, logins (and login emails), settings and the audit trail stay, and live sending stays armed or disarmed as it was. With **Put injects already sent back on the schedule** (ticked), injects sent, failed or overdue during testing are scheduled again at their send time (those whose time has passed go overdue: send them by hand or change their time). Received email from before the reset is never synced back.

Neither can be undone: download a backup first.

## 7. Safety controls
- **Live sending switch** (Settings): disarmed = nothing reaches teams; arming needs `SEND LIVE`. A restored backup always comes back disarmed.
- **Pause all sending:** stops live and test deliveries immediately, even halfway through an inject's teams; resuming never delivers anything twice.
- **Overdue instead of late:** an inject more than the grace period past its time (after an outage, or arming late) is held as **overdue**. Send it by hand or reschedule it.
- **No duplicates:** every delivery is recorded before it happens; an in-app message is created in the same transaction that marks it delivered.
- **Confirmations:** typing the inject id for a manual live send or a deletion; `RESTORE` for a restore.
- **Separation:** participant logins can only see their own team's mailbox. They cannot open injects, schedules, rubrics, grades, other teams, or this manual.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Scheduler: … is the worker running?" | The scheduler worker is down. Check the *scheduler* component in DigitalOcean; meanwhile send urgent injects by hand (in-app sends deliver immediately). |
| "Too many attempts" at sign-in | 10 wrong passwords for one login lock that login for 10 minutes on that network; other logins (and ExCon) still work. Reset the password if they have lost it. |
| A participant can't sign in | Check their **activity** page (People & logins, next to their username) for failed sign-ins and the device. Then People & logins → **reset password**, give them the new one (and its slip, with a new QR code: the old one stops working). "This sign-in code no longer works": the slip is older than 14 days or the password was reset; print a new **slip**. Check the login isn't disabled and the person has a team. |
| "You are signed out" in SIMEX Mail | Their session ended (password reset, signed out in another tab, or 14 days old). They sign in again in a new tab and come back: what they were writing is still there; press Send again. |
| A team says nothing arrived | Team inboxes → View as team. If it's there, they may be looking at another folder or another team's login. |
| Inject **overdue** | Send now, or move its time and Schedule. |
| An inject went out with a missing attachment, link or a mistake | Open it → **Send a follow-up** (5.3): add the file, explain, send it in the same conversation. |
| Inject **failed / partial** | Open it, read the error, fix it, "only retry the failed deliveries". One inject failing never holds up the others. |
| An email reply is missing or shows no text | The scheduler checks Resend every minute for received email the webhook missed (last 2 hours) and retries failed fetches. For older mail, Replies → **Sync email from Resend**. |
| A reply is "unmatched" or a "guess" | Open it and set the inject and team under **Match**. |
| Email channel: "Mailer: log" | `RESEND_API_KEY` is missing; email injects are only logged. In-app is unaffected. |
| Emails aren't arriving / bouncing | Log → the email → delivery status. If it can't be fixed quickly, switch to SIMEX Mail (5.6). |
| AI analysis says "No provider configured" or calls fail | The web and scheduler components need `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, or `AI_PROVIDER=bedrock` with `AWS_BEARER_TOKEN_BEDROCK` (and `AWS_REGION`). The last error is on Settings → AI analysis. Grading by hand is unaffected. |
| AI analysis stopped for the day | The daily cap was reached: raise it on Settings → AI analysis. |
| "… has exercise mail or grades: make it inactive" when deleting a team | Deleting would take its inbox, replies and grades with it. Untick **active** on People & teams instead (it stops getting injects). |
| Need to undo a big mistake | Settings → Restore a backup (type RESTORE). A backup of this app replaces everything, including logins. A backup from the first (email-only) version of the app is merged in instead: settings, senders, roster, injects with attachments, rubrics and grades. |

## 9. Quick reference: a day of the exercise
1. Status bar OK (scheduler recent).
2. Today's injects scheduled, times right, no warnings.
3. Teams signed in. Arm live sending.
4. Watch **Replies**, **Dashboard** and **Team inboxes**; answer in character; grade as answers arrive.
5. Handle overdue or failed injects; pause if anything looks wrong.
6. End of day: disarm, download a backup, grade the day's replies.

**End of the exercise:** disarm, backup, finish grading, compile the reports, edit them (with each team's leader), rewrite the ExCon report, print, debrief (section 6).
