# SIMEX Mail: quick guide

SIMEX Mail is your team's mailbox for the exercise. Everything here is **exercise material**, not a real emergency. Messages from the scenario's organisations (government, OCHA, media, partners…) arrive here, and you answer them here.

## Signing in
- **Scan the QR code on your login slip** with your phone's camera: you are signed in, and SIMEX Mail shows the one step left to get alerts on that phone. On a laptop, open the address on the slip and sign in with your username and password.
- Keep your slip: it is your login. Don't share it with other teams.

## Your mailbox
- **Two tabs at the top: your team's inbox, and My mail.**
  - **Team inbox:** one mailbox per team. Everyone in your team sees the same mail. What you send from here appears in everyone's **Sent**, with your name on it.
  - **My mail:** your own mailbox, at your own address (e.g. *jane.doe@a.teams…*). It gets a copy of everything sent to your team, plus what your teammates and your team leader write to you personally. Starring, labels or reading here don't change the team inbox, and the other way round. A red number on a tab is its unread mail.
  - **From:** at the top of every message you write, choose whether it goes from your **team's address** (blue) or **your own address** (green). It starts on the team's in the team inbox and on yours in My mail. What goes from the team's address is in the team inbox's Sent for everyone; what goes from yours is in your My mail.
  - You can write to a teammate (pick their name), copy teammates in, or write to the whole team from your own address (pick your team: it lands in the team inbox and in everyone's My mail). When you answer an organisation (a persona) from My mail, the answer also appears in the team inbox's **Sent**, so your team and ExCon always see every answer; teammates you copy in get it in their My mail.
- Keep the page open during the exercise. New mail appears on its own; the tab title shows the number of unread conversations, e.g. **Inbox (3)**.
- **Alerts for new mail:** when a message arrives you hear a chime and see a blue bar at the bottom of the page (click **Open**), the tab shows the number of unread conversations and a red badge, and its title blinks while you are in another tab. On your first visit, click **Turn on alerts** in the blue banner so your browser can also show a notification on your screen when this tab is in the background. Keep the SIMEX Mail tab open all day. The **speaker** at the top right mutes or unmutes alerts (blue when on, crossed out when muted). If your browser has blocked notifications, allow them for this site in its address-bar settings.
- You are alerted to both: new mail for the team and mail to you personally, whichever tab is open.
- **Notifications with SIMEX Mail closed:** once you have turned on alerts, new mail is announced on your computer or phone even when SIMEX Mail isn't open (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Android).
- **On an iPhone or iPad,** notifications only work from the home screen: in Safari tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. Open **SIMEX Mail** from your home screen (if you scanned your slip, you're signed in already; otherwise sign in), and tap **Turn on alerts**, then **Allow**. You then get a notification for every new message, like a mail app. Focus / Do Not Disturb can hide them.

## Reading
- **Inbox** lists conversations, newest first. **Bold** = unread. A number after the sender = messages in the conversation. A paperclip = attachments.
- Click a conversation to open it. Earlier messages are folded: click one to unfold it. Click **to me ▾** to see all the details (from, to, date).
- **Attachments** are at the bottom of a message: click one to download it.
- **Search** (top bar) looks through subjects, text and senders.
- **Dates and times are the scenario's.** When ExCon runs the simulation clock, the calendar and clock at the top show the date and time in the exercise scenario, and every message is dated in that timeline, not by the real clock.
- Folders on the left: **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **All mail** (everything, including archived), **Trash**.
- Tidy up with the icons that appear when you point at a conversation, or tick several and use the toolbar (on a phone, tap the round picture next to a conversation to select it; while some are selected, tapping a conversation selects it too): **archive** (out of the Inbox, still in All mail), **delete** (to Trash, restorable), **mark as read / unread**, **star**.

## Labels (your own folders)
- **Create a label:** click **+** next to **Labels** on the left, type a name (e.g. "Logistics", "Media") and click **Create**. Labels belong to your team: everyone in the team sees the same ones.
- **Move to** (folder icon): files the conversation under a label and takes it out of the Inbox, like moving it to a folder. **Labels** (tag icon): adds or removes labels and leaves the conversation where it is; a conversation can have several. Both work on an open conversation or on the ones you've ticked in the list, and both have **Create new** at the bottom.
- You can also **drag** conversations from the list onto a label (or back onto **Inbox**).
- Click a label on the left to see its conversations; the number next to it is how many are unread. New replies in a labelled conversation still arrive in your Inbox, with the label on them.
- Remove a label from one conversation with the **×** on it at the top of the conversation. Point at a label on the left and click **⋮** to change its colour, rename it (**Edit**) or **Remove label** (its conversations stay in **All mail**).

## Replying
- Open the conversation and click **Reply** (to the sender), **Reply all** (everyone on it) or **Forward**, at the bottom or on a message.
- Write your answer and click **Send**. Attach files with the **paperclip** next to Send (up to the limit shown if too large).

## Writing a new message
- Click **Compose** (top left; on a phone, the button at the bottom right). A *New Message* window opens at the bottom right; you can minimise it or make it full screen.
- **Which mailbox you are in** is named at the top, next to SIMEX Mail, each with its own colour: your **team inbox** (blue, shared by everyone in your team) or **My mail** (green, your own). Check it before you write: what you send from the team inbox goes from the team's address.
- **To:** your address book fills up as you go, as in any webmail: everyone who has written to you or whom you have written to, the most recent first. Start typing a name or an address and pick from the list. To write to someone for the first time, type their **full address** (e.g. from a document or a signature) and press Enter: if they are in the exercise, they are added, and stay in your address book. An address shown in red isn't in SIMEX Mail: check it. **Cc / Bcc** add more recipients.
- **Your team leader** is in the list too (e.g. "UNDAC Team A TL"): write to them, or copy them in, like anyone else. Their answers arrive in your Inbox.
- Add a **subject** and your message, attach files if needed, and click **Send**.
- Drafts are saved automatically every few seconds; find them under **Drafts** and click one to continue.

## Calendar
- Open **Calendar** from the **grid of nine dots** at the top right (the app drawer, with your mailboxes and this guide). Use it to keep track of the meetings and calls your team arranges.
- Two calendars, on the left: your **team's calendar**, which everyone in your team sees and can change, and **My calendar**, your own. Tick or untick them to show or hide them.
- **Create** (or drag across the hours) adds an event: a title, when, how (in person, phone, VTC), where and a description, and which calendar it goes on. Click an event to see, edit or delete it; drag it to move it, or its bottom edge to change how long it lasts.
- **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Schedule** (a list) at the top; **Today** and the arrows move through the days. Times are the scenario's, like your mail.
- **Guests:** in the event, add guests as in Compose (a name from your address book, or someone's exact address): teammates, your team, the organisations (personas). Each gets an invitation by mail from that calendar's mailbox. A change reaches them as an **Updated invitation**, and deleting the event sends a **Cancelled** mail.
- **An invitation you get** has a card above the text, marked **Invitation** in the list: click **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No** (with a note if you like). Your answer goes back to the organiser by mail, and the event is on your calendar (dashed until you answer). You can answer on the calendar too.
- **Create event** (the calendar icon at the top of a conversation) starts an event from that conversation, with the people in it as guests.

## Shortcuts (when you're not typing)
`c` compose · `/` search · `v` move to · `l` labels · `j` / `k` next / previous · `Enter` open · `r` reply · `a` reply all · `f` forward · `e` archive · `#` delete · `u` back to the list · `Esc` close the compose window.

## What ExCon sees
Your team leader and ExCon can read your team inbox and your My mail (read-only: they never mark anything read for you). SIMEX Mail records what your team does in it, for the debrief: who reads and writes what and when, which files you open, and how you organise the mailbox (stars, labels, archive, searches, drafts). Your team's debrief report describes how the team handled its mail, without naming anyone.

## Problems
Can't sign in, or something looks wrong? Ask ExCon. They can reset your password. Don't share your login with other teams.
