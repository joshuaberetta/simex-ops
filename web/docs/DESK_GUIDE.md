# SIMEX Mail for the persona desk

You are on the **persona desk**: part of ExCon, playing the exercise's personas (the press, the government, the agencies...) for every team. Several people can be on the desk at once: you all share **one mailbox**. Everything here is **exercise material**, not a real emergency.

## The tabs at the top
- **Persona desk**: the shared mailbox. Its **Inbox** has every message a team writes to a persona (in SIMEX Mail or by email); its **Sent**, everything the personas sent the teams (the injects, ExCon's follow-ups and answers, the AI personas' answers and what the desk writes). The red number is unread conversations.
- **Teams & team leaders** (read-only): each team's inbox, its team leader's mailbox and (when members have their own) each member's, exactly as they see them. Opening mail there does **not** mark it read for them. The side bar switches between teams.

## Telling the personas apart
- Every conversation shows a **coloured tag for each persona** in it and a grey tag for the **team** on the other side (and **Test** for the Test team). The colours are the same everywhere: the tag, the coloured edge of the row and of each message, the dot in the side bar and the **From** of your message. ExCon can change a persona's colour on the Senders tab.
- In a conversation, each message from a persona says who sent it: **Inject**, **Follow-up (ExCon)**, **Sent by** a desk member, **Sent by ExCon** or **Written by the AI persona**. A team's message shows which persona it went **to**.
- On the left, **Personas** shows one persona's conversations and **Teams** one team's, each with its unread conversations. **AI** marks personas that answer on their own.

## Answering a team
- Open the conversation and click **Reply**. **From** is already the persona the team wrote to; **To** is that team. Your answer lands in the team's conversation, in SIMEX Mail or by email, like any other answer from that persona. It is also recorded as the persona's answer on ExCon's Replies tab.
- You can change **From** to have another persona write in the same conversation (e.g. the press picking up on a government message). The team sees it in the same conversation.
- If an AI persona was going to answer that conversation, your answer replaces its answer: its queued answer is cancelled.
- **Someone else on it?** At the top of a conversation, a yellow bar says who else on the desk has it open, and a red one who is writing an answer, so two of you don't answer the same message. Read and starred are shared: a conversation one of you opened is no longer unread for the others.

## Writing first
Click **Write as a persona**, choose **From** (each persona with its address), and pick one or more teams in To, Cc or Bcc: each team gets its own message and only ever sees its own. New messages to the teams only go out once ExCon has armed live sending (except to the Test team), and nothing goes while sending is paused.

## What you can't do here
- Write as the **team leader persona**: each team's leader plays it themselves.
- Change anything in a team's mailbox, or reach ExCon's screens (injects, grading, settings).
- Answer a message from an email address ExCon hasn't matched to a team yet. ExCon answers those from the Replies tab.

## Alerts
Click **Turn on alerts** on each device: a chime and a notification for each new message to a persona, even with SIMEX Mail in the background (on an iPhone, from the home-screen app: **Share → Add to Home Screen**).

## Problems
Ask ExCon. If a page looks stuck, reload it.
