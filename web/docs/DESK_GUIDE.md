# SIMEX Mail for the persona desk

You are on the **persona desk**: part of ExCon, playing the exercise's personas (the press, the government, the agencies...) for every team. Several people can be on the desk at once: you all share **one mailbox**. Everything here is **exercise material**, not a real emergency.

## The tabs at the top
- **Persona desk**: the shared mailbox. Its **Inbox** has every message a team writes to a persona (in SIMEX Mail or by email); its **Sent**, everything the personas sent the teams (the injects, ExCon's follow-ups and answers, the AI personas' answers and what the desk writes). The red number is unread conversations.
- **Teams & team leaders** (read-only): each team's inbox, its team leader's mailbox and (when members have their own) each member's, exactly as they see them. Opening mail there does **not** mark it read for them. The side bar switches between teams.
- **Dashboard** and **Assign**: how the desk is keeping up, and who has which conversations (below).
- **ChatSIMEX** (on the right): ask SIMEX AI (below).

## ChatSIMEX: ask before you answer
Click **ChatSIMEX** at the right of the tabs to open a chat beside the mail, on any desk page. Ask in your own words, for example:
- *Who is Salma?* A team is writing to a persona, or about someone, you haven't played. You get a briefing: who they are, how to play them, what they know, what they have told each team so far, what is still to come (don't give it away), and which conversations with them are open now.
- *What has been said about the bridge?* What the personas and the teams wrote about it, team by team, and what the teams said among themselves.
- *How do I answer this?* On a conversation, what the team asks, what the persona can say now, what the persona told the other teams, what to hold back, and a draft. **Use in reply** opens your reply with it. Read the **Check before sending** warnings.

SIMEX AI searches the desk's mailbox, the teams' mailboxes, the injects, the role plays and the Library, and knows who is actually playing in each team (ExCon's roster: *who is on Team A?*), and says where each team stands on each (*has it*, *NOT YET*). It takes about a minute. Two rules it follows, and so should you: **teams never cross** (use what another team was told to keep a persona consistent, never to pass it on), and **a persona can't know what a team said among itself**. Your chats are yours only: nobody else on the desk, or in ExCon, sees them. The answers can quote scripts and planned injects: never paste them to a team. **New chat** starts afresh, **History** opens earlier ones.

**Simulation time.** When ExCon runs the simulation clock, the calendar and clock at the top show the scenario's date and time, with the real time beside it. Messages show the real time, with their simulation time under it (marked with a small clock): the teams see only the simulation time, so use that one when a persona mentions a time.

## Telling the personas apart
- Every conversation shows a **coloured tag for each persona** in it and a grey tag for the **team** on the other side (and **Test** for the Test team). The colours are the same everywhere: the tag, the coloured edge of the row and of each message, the dot in the side bar and the **From** of your message. ExCon can change a persona's colour on the Senders tab.
- In a conversation, each message from a persona says who sent it: **Inject**, **Follow-up (ExCon)**, **Sent by** a desk member, **Sent by ExCon** or **Written by the AI persona**. A team's message shows which persona it went **to**.
- On the left, **Personas** shows one persona's conversations and **Teams** one team's, each with its unread conversations. **AI** marks personas that answer on their own.

## The Dashboard tab
How the desk is keeping up, refreshed every minute: what needs attention and how many nobody has taken, how long the teams wait, how fast you answer, and an hourly strip of messages in against answers out. **Waiting longest** lists the teams waiting, with who has each. **Requests to the personas** shows what the teams asked each persona, unanswered first; **Decisions and commitments** what they told a persona they will do (a cue for a follow-up), and **Figures and facts** the numbers and places they gave (keep the personas' answers consistent with them). **Teams** shows whether one is being left behind, **Personas** which ones carry the load (time for a second person?), **Who is answering** who is online and what each has taken. **Who has which conversations** lists each of you with what you have: how many, how many need attention, the longest wait, follow-ups, and the conversations themselves (open a name). **Nobody has them** shows the open ones nobody has taken. Requests, commitments and flags are SIMEX AI's reading of the teams' messages: a hint, check the message.

## Answering a team
- Open the conversation and click **Reply**. **From** is already the persona the team wrote to; **To** is that team. Your answer lands in the team's conversation, in SIMEX Mail or by email, like any other answer from that persona. It is also recorded as the persona's answer on ExCon's Replies tab.
- You can change **From** to have another persona write in the same conversation (e.g. the press picking up on a government message). The team sees it in the same conversation.
- **Suggest a reply** (under the bar at the top of a conversation): SIMEX AI looks up the documents, injects and role plays the team has had so far and suggests the persona's answer, with what it rests on, what the team got wrong, what isn't in the package and what is still to come for this team (so you don't give it away early). Add what the answer should do if you like (*keep it short*), wait about a minute, then **Reply with this** and read it before you send it. The notes can quote role-play scripts: they are for the desk, never paste them to a team.
- If an AI persona was going to answer that conversation, your answer replaces its answer: its queued answer is cancelled.
- **Someone else on it?** At the top of a conversation, a yellow bar says who else on the desk has it open, and a red one who is writing an answer, so two of you don't answer the same message. Read and starred are shared: a conversation one of you opened is no longer unread for the others.

## What still needs an answer
Several of you share the desk, so *read* doesn't mean *answered*. The **Inbox** comes in sections:
- **Needs attention**: a team is waiting for a persona's answer (*Waiting 12 min*), a follow-up is due, or an AI persona's answer was held. The longest waiting first; **Urgent** ones above. The red box on the left says how many.
- **Probably no reply needed (SIMEX AI)**: SIMEX AI reads the team's latest message as a thanks or an acknowledgement. Check it, then **No reply needed**.
- **Later**: follow-ups not due yet, and conversations an AI persona is about to answer.
- **Answered or nothing to answer**: the rest.

Prefer your Inbox like any email, newest at the top? Click **In sections · show newest first** above the list (and **Newest first · show in sections** to go back); your choice stays as you move around.

A conversation leaves Needs attention as soon as a persona answers that team in it; a new message from the team puts it back. A blue tag on a row is what SIMEX AI reads the team asking for: a hint, check the message.

At the top of each conversation:
- **I'll take it**: the others see your name on its row (**Yours** for you), so two of you don't pick up the same one. **Release** when you hand it back. Rows also show who is reading or writing in a conversation right now.
- **Assign to…**: give it to someone else on the desk (or to ExCon). They get a notification and find it under **Assigned to me**; if someone had it, they are told too.
- **Follow** / **Unfollow**: hear about this conversation even when someone else has it (or stop hearing about it).
- **No reply needed**: nothing to answer (a thanks, a confirmation). It leaves Needs attention and the next waiting conversation opens. **Reopen** undoes it.
- **Follow up** now or in 15 minutes to 4 hours, with a note (*chase the convoy ETA*): it waits in Later, comes back to Needs attention when due, and ends when a persona writes in it (or **Stop**).
- **Ask SIMEX AI**, when SIMEX AI hasn't read it yet; **Next waiting** to move on.

Tick several rows in the Inbox for **I'll take these**, **Assign to…**, **No reply needed** or **Reopen** at once; the ✓ on a row (on hover) is **No reply needed**.

## Your conversations and your notifications
- On the left, **Assigned to me** has the conversations you took or were given, with what needs attention first; its number is how many of them need attention. **Following** has the ones you answered in, set a follow-up on or follow, but don't have.
- The **bell** at the top (next to ?) is yours alone. It tells you when:
  - a conversation is given to you, or someone takes over or reassigns one you had;
  - one of your follow-ups falls due;
  - a team writes again in a conversation you have or follow;
  - someone else answers in it, or an AI persona's answer to it is held or failed;
  - a team has waited too long in one of yours.
- While nobody has a conversation, everyone who answered in it or set a follow-up on it hears when the team writes again. Once someone has it, only they do, plus anyone who clicked **Follow**.
- Click a notification to open the conversation. **✕** dismisses it; **Mark all read** and **Clear all** are at the top. Conversations given to you, follow-ups due and teams waiting too long also come to your phone, if you turned on alerts.

## The Assign tab
**Board**: the conversations that still need something, in columns: **Not assigned** first, then one for each of you (a green dot: online now), with how many each has, how many need attention and the longest wait, and the personas they have by default. Each card shows the team, the persona, how long the team has waited, *Urgent*, what SIMEX AI reads the team as asking, and who has it open now; click its subject to open it.
- **Drag a card onto someone** to give it to them (onto **Not assigned** to release it). Click several cards first to move them together (Esc clears the choice); they are told once for all of them. On a phone or tablet, drag by the dots at the card's top left, or use the menu at the bottom of each card.
- Choose a team, a persona, or *and answered, last 4 h* to see answered ones too. **ExCon logins** adds columns for ExCon staff (those with a conversation always show). The board keeps itself up to date.

**Default assignments**: who has each persona's conversations with each team, so nobody has to hand them out. One row per persona: **All teams** covers every team without its own choice, and a team's cell overrides it (CCPRR: All teams to Sam, Team B and Team D to Alex). **Whole row to…** gives every team to one person; **Clear the row** removes the row's choices. Changes save as you make them.
- A conversation goes to its default person when it starts: when the persona first writes to the team (an inject: quietly, they hear when the team writes) or when the team first writes to the persona. *Yours by the default assignments* shows on its bar, *by default* on its card.
- Never over someone: a conversation anyone took, was given or released by hand keeps that. **Apply to open conversations nobody has** gives those to their default person now.
- Someone who leaves the desk is skipped: their conversations go to the **All teams** choice, or nobody.

## Writing first
Click **Write as a persona**, choose **From** (each persona with its address), and pick one or more teams, team members (e.g. **Team A (Jane Doe)**) or team leaders (**Team A leader (John Roe)**) in To, Cc or Bcc: start typing a team, a name or an address and every match is suggested. Each gets its own message and only ever sees its own. A member's or a leader's goes to their own mailbox, not the team's. A leader can't answer a persona in SIMEX Mail (only by email). New messages to the teams only go out once ExCon has armed live sending (except to the Test team), and nothing goes while sending is paused.

## What you can't do here
- Write as the **team leader persona**: each team's leader plays it themselves.
- Change anything in a team's mailbox, or reach ExCon's screens (injects, grading, settings).
- Answer a message from an email address ExCon hasn't matched to a team yet. ExCon answers those from the Replies tab.

## Alerts
Click **Turn on alerts** on each device: a chime and a notification for each new message to a persona, even with SIMEX Mail in the background (on an iPhone, from the home-screen app: **Share → Add to Home Screen**).

## Problems
Ask ExCon. If a page looks stuck, reload it.
