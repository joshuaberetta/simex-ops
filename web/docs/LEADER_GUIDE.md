# SIMEX Mail for team leaders

You are your team's **team leader**: part of ExCon, not of the team. You play the team leader persona (e.g. *UNDAC Team A TL*) in the exercise, follow how your team works and help it along. Everything here is **exercise material**, not a real emergency.

## The three tabs at the top
- **My mail**: your own mailbox, used like any webmail (Compose, labels, stars, search, alerts).
- **Team inbox** (read-only): exactly what your team has, so you can follow its work. Opening mail here does **not** mark it read for the team. The red number is new mail in the team's inbox since you last looked.
- **Dashboard**: your team's replies in figures, and Claude's reading of them.

You only ever see your own team. The other teams are independent of yours.

## My mail
- **Sent** holds everything sent as the team leader persona: the injects ExCon schedules in your name, ExCon's answers in your name, and what you write yourself. **Inbox** holds what your team writes to the team leader persona (also when they copy you in), and the injects ExCon sends to the team leaders too.
- **Writing to your team:** click **Compose** and pick your team, or open a conversation and click **Reply**. Your message reaches the team's inbox from the team leader persona, in the same conversation. You can only write to your own team.
- **An inject sent to you as well** (from another persona) can't be answered from here: use **Forward to** your team if you want to pass it on.
- Your answers to the team count as the persona's answer: ExCon sees them on the Replies tab ("answered by the team leader"), so it won't answer the same message twice.

## Team inbox
- It opens on **Team replies**: the conversations your team has written in, newest reply first, with the name of the member who wrote the latest message. **Inbox**, **Sent** and **All mail** are there too.
- In a conversation, each message from the team shows who wrote it, how many minutes after the message it answers, and, once Claude has read it: its tone, a quality score (clarity, completeness, actionability, out of 5), flags, and whether it **may be AI-written**. Click **Claude's reading** for the summary and the reasons.
- "Likely / possibly AI-assisted" is Claude's estimate from the text and the time taken to write it. Short messages and non-native English make it unreliable: a reason to look closer, never proof. Don't confront a participant on it alone. Participants never see any of this.

## Dashboard
Messages written, injects answered, median time to the first reply, late or missing replies, delivered → read → answered, who in the team is writing, replies per hour, and Claude's reading: tone, quality, possibly AI-written replies with the reasons, flags, what the team asks for and what it commits to. Click a time to open the conversation in the Team inbox. The page refreshes every two minutes.

## Alerts
As in the team's SIMEX Mail: a chime and a blue bar for new mail in **My mail**, and, once you click **Turn on alerts**, notifications on your computer or phone (on an iPhone, from the home-screen app: **Share → Add to Home Screen**).

## Problems
Ask ExCon. If a page looks stuck, reload it.
