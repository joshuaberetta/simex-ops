Senders

The personas injects come from, and the address book participants write to. The username identifies the persona (injects use it) and is its email address, username@{{ MAIL_DOMAIN }}: email can only be sent from that domain. In SIMEX Mail a persona can show any address, e.g. news@cnn.com and news@reuters.com for the usernames cnn-news and reuters-news; empty parts default to the username and {{ MAIL_DOMAIN }}. {team} in a username, and {team_name} in a name, are filled in per team. Reply-to only matters for the email channel (leave it empty so replies are tracked). The team leader persona (e.g. undac-{team}-tl) is played by each team's leader in SIMEX Mail: what is sent as it is in the leader's Sent, and the team's mail to it reaches the leader's Inbox.

Import a list of senders

A spreadsheet (.xlsx) or CSV with a header row: Alias (the address in SIMEX Mail, e.g. who@epeigonia.org), optionally Username (else the alias's username), Display name, Organisation (shown to teams), Organisation / use (ExCon's notes; a plain Organisation column is read as these), Category and Active (yes / no). A row is the sender with that username (or, without a Username column, that address); the others are added. Empty cells leave a value as it is, and usernames are never renamed. You see the plan, with every value it would change, before anything changes. Download the senders in this format to edit and import them again.

Team and member addresses

The addresses the teams and their members have in SIMEX Mail, as the personas have theirs: the team's shared inbox, and each member's own mailbox (My mail). {team} is the team code; for members, {name} is their full name (jane.doe), {first} and {last} their first and last names. A member whose address is taken already (a namesake, a team, a persona) gets their number after it. Empty parts use the defaults shown. SIMEX Mail only: nothing is emailed to these addresses, and mail already sent keeps the address it had.
