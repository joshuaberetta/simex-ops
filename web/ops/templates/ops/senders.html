Senders

The personas injects come from, and the address book participants write to. The username identifies the persona (injects use it) and is its email address, username@{{ MAIL_DOMAIN }}: email can only be sent from that domain. In SIMEX Mail a persona can show any address, e.g. news@cnn.com and news@reuters.com for the usernames cnn-news and reuters-news; empty parts default to the username and {{ MAIL_DOMAIN }}. {team} in a username, and {team_name} in a name, are filled in per team. Reply-to only matters for the email channel (leave it empty so replies are tracked).
