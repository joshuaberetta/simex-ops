Who sees what

Team members see only their own team's inbox in SIMEX Mail at {{ MAIL_URL }}: messages delivered to the team, what the team sent, and the persona address book. They never see injects, schedules, rubrics, grades or other teams.

Team leaders sign in at the same address. They play the team leader persona (Senders tab): they have their own mailbox (what is sent as that persona is in their Sent, the team's mail to it in their Inbox, and injects marked "also to team leaders"), they write only to their own team, read the team's inbox read-only with Claude's reading of each reply, and see their team's Dashboard. Never other teams, injects, rubrics or grades.

ExCon logins are Django staff users (or members of the excon group); create more in Django admin.
