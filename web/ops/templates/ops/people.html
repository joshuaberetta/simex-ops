CSV lines: name,email,team code,role (email optional, role tl or empty). Unknown team codes create a team. Existing people (same email) are updated.

Who sees what

Participant logins see only their own team's inbox at /inbox/: messages delivered to the team, what the team sent, and the persona address book. They never see injects, schedules, rubrics, grades or other teams.

ExCon logins are Django staff users (or members of the excon group); create more in Django admin.
