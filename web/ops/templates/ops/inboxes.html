Team inboxes

"View as team" opens the participants' mail client exactly as that team sees it, read-only: nothing is marked read and nothing can be sent. "Team leader" does the same for the mailbox of the team's leader (ExCon), who plays the team leader persona, and each member's name for that member's own mailbox (with personal mailboxes on, Settings). The figures are the team inbox's.

Clear a team's mailbox

After testing: deletes everything in the team's mailbox (received, sent, drafts), its replies on the Replies tab with their AI analysis, the persona answers to them and its grades. Deliveries stay in the Log. This can't be undone: download a backup first (Settings).
