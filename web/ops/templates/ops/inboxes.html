Team inboxes

Open the persona desk Meetings the shared mailbox where the teams' messages to the personas arrive and from which you write as any persona (People & logins, role Persona desk, for role-players without access to these screens)

"View as team" opens the participants' mail client exactly as that team sees it, read-only: nothing is marked read and nothing can be sent. "Team leader" does the same for the mailbox of the team's leader (ExCon), who plays the team leader persona, and each member's name for that member's own mailbox (with personal mailboxes on, Settings). The figures are the team inbox's.

Clear mailboxes

After testing. Every mailbox deletes everything in the team's inbox, its leader's mailbox and each member's own (received, sent, drafts, labels), its replies on the Replies tab with their AI analysis, the persona answers to them, its grades and the persona desk's copies. Members' own mailboxes only empties just the members' My mail, leaving the team inbox and its replies. Deliveries stay in the Log, and count on the Dashboard, unless you tick the box. To clear the whole exercise before going live (every team, the Dashboard, reports, test runs, Log), use Settings → Start afresh. This can't be undone: download a backup first (Settings).
