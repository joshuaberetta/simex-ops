1. Injects

Upload MEL-MIL.xlsx or an injects .json export. Only email injects are imported, and only the fields participants see (send time, sender, recipients, subject, content, document names). Expected outcomes, story, theme, key figures and the ExCon description are dropped and never stored. New injects are delivered in-app; switch any to email on the inject page or in bulk on the list.

You will see what changes before anything is saved. Injects already sent are never touched; fields you edited here are kept unless you choose to overwrite them.

2. Inject documents

Upload a .zip of the inject documents (e.g. the SIMEX 4. Injects folder). Files are matched to injects by the names in the MEL's Supporting documents column; a file with the same name replaces the earlier one. Max 30 MB per file. Matching runs in the background after the upload: you can leave this page, and its progress is shown here.
