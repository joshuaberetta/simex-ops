Personas the teams can write to that answer on their own, like non-player characters: SIMEX AI writes each answer in character from the persona's profile (who they are, what they know and may share, their limits), after a delay drawn from its timing, in the same conversation, in SIMEX Mail or by email. Put only what the teams may learn in a profile: never control-side material. Answers go out as the persona and are on the Replies tab; team leaders see their team's conversations with them.

A profile also shapes Suggest a reply and ChatSIMEX on the persona desk, even while it doesn't answer on its own: give every persona the desk plays one, then switch any of them to answer on its own when the desk needs a hand.

Import profiles

A spreadsheet or CSV, one persona per row by its Username: Organisation, Role, Personality and voice, What they know and may share, Boundaries, Timing, Office hours from / to, Sends straight away, Answers on its own. Empty cells leave a value as it is. You see what it adds and changes first.

Export gives the profiles in these columns, then a row for each persona without one: fill them in and import it back. Only personas on the Senders tab.
