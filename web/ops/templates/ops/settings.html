Simulation clock

SIMEX Mail dates every message at the scenario's date and time instead of the real one, and shows a calendar and clock of the simulation time at the top. Team members see only the simulation time; team leaders, the persona desk and ExCon see both. An inject's delivery is dated at its Scenario time (if it has one); everything else at the clock's time when it arrives. Mail keeps the date it got: changing the clock later doesn't re-date it. Email is not affected.
