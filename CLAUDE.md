# SIMEX ops

**Production is the Django app in `web/`** (`.do/app.yaml`: web + `run_scheduler` worker, https://ops.simex.kobolabs.dev). It delivers injects by email (Resend) or, as the backup, in the in-app SIMEX Mail inboxes; `conf.channel_mode` (Settings → Delivery channel: email | inapp | per_inject) decides, read through `Inject.effective_channel` (never `inj.channel` directly for delivery decisions). Tests: `cd web && .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`. Keep `web/docs/MANUAL.md` (served at /help/) in step with any screen or flow change. Participants use SIMEX Mail at `MAIL_URL` (https://mail.simex.kobolabs.dev, same app and session cookie; `ops.middleware.MailHost` keeps that host to `/inbox/` and its sign-in, `templates/ops/mail/login.html`, and sends ExCon paths back to `PUBLIC_URL`). New inbox mail is Web-Pushed (`ops/push.py`, after commit, in a thread; service worker `templates/ops/mail/sw.js`, manifest at `/inbox/manifest.webmanifest`) to the team's subscribed devices, sender and subject only; key `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, tests use `push.set_sender(fake)`. Participant pages (`ops/views_inbox.py`, `templates/ops/mail/`) must filter every query by the viewer's mailbox: a mailbox is `(team, owner)` (`Mail.owner` / `MailLabel.owner`: None = the team's shared inbox, a Person = that team leader's own or, with `conf.personal_mailboxes`, a member's own at `/inbox/me/`), always through `Viewer.box` / `v.mails()` / `v.labels()`; teams never cross. A member's own mailbox (`Viewer.member_box`; address `views_inbox.member_address`, book key `m:<person id>`; team and member addresses come from `ops/addresses.py`, patterns set on the Senders tab, `conf` `addr_*`, deduplicated against personas, teams and namesakes, the old defaults still recognised on older mail) gets a copy of every Mail landing in the team inbox (`delivery.to_members`, same thread and Message, no push; only theirs if `to_person`; an `each_person` inject goes straight to each member's own mailbox instead, `Message.inbox_owner`, never the team inbox, `render.build_inapp`) and personal mail between teammates and with the leader; an answer to a persona from it is also copied to the team inbox's Sent, and that copy is the `Response.mail`. SIMEX Mail names a member "Team A (Jane Doe)" (`addresses.member_name`: From, To/Cc, the address book). A member picks the From of each message (`views_inbox.from_choices` / `sender_box`: the team's address or their own; from the team's, a copy in their own Sent too). In a leader's box, team mail to them gets the `MailLabel.auto` label `team` or `member` (`views_inbox.auto_label`). The leader writes to the team (a delivered Message) or to chosen members only (`delivery.deliver_personal`, a Message straight to their boxes), and reads members' boxes read-only (`/inbox/members/<pid>/`, Team members tab, `views_leader.members`); ExCon at `/inbox/as/<team>/member/<pid>/`. Every ExCon view is `@excon_required` and every team-leader-only view `@leader_required` (tests walk the URLconf). Roles (`models.ROLES`): `member` = participant sharing the team inbox; `tl` = the team's one leader, from ExCon, never a participant (use `Person.objects.participants()` wherever people get injects, push or are matched to replies). A member may also hold a `TeamRole` (`Person.team_role`, e.g. Deputy Team Leader; People & logins → Team roles, or the roster's role column): a label only, never for leaders, changing nothing in SIMEX Mail; the Dashboards (`views_ai.by_team_role`) and reports (`report.by_team_role`, `inbox.members[].team_role`) break figures down by it. The leader plays the team leader persona (`Sender.team_leader`, `render.leader_persona`): `delivery._put_mail` puts whatever is sent as it in the leader's Sent (same thread id in both boxes), members' mail to it also lands in the leader's Inbox, the leader's own sends are `Message`s (kind `reply` with `response` when answering, else `leader`; `author` = the leader), and `Inject.to_leaders` adds a copy to each leader's box (`Message.inbox_owner`). Leaders read the other team leaders' mailboxes read-only at `/inbox/leaders/<team id>/` (`Viewer` box `peer`, `views_inbox.peer_teams`; never another team's inbox), and their team's inbox read-only at `/inbox/team/` with Claude's reading of the replies and documents (and an AI? chip on list rows, `views_inbox.ai_flags`, for `Viewer.sees_ai` only: the team's leader in the team inbox and members' boxes, ExCon staff in `/inbox/as/`; never members, peers or the desk) and their team's Dashboard (`ops/views_leader.py`); their copies are left out of the team's delivery and read figures. Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-ins of people with a `Person` are `LoginEvent`s (`ops/activity.py`, from Django's auth signals; `request.signin_method = "qr"` before `login()` for the QR link; kept through Start afresh) and `Person.last_seen_at` is stamped at most once a minute by `middleware.LastSeen`; People & logins shows each one's status and links to `/people/<pid>/activity/` (`activity.timeline`: sign-ins, `MailAction`s, read/download `Event`s, Responses and authored Messages). Login slips carry a QR code (`ops/signin.py`: a signed, stateless token for a member's, leader's or persona desk member's login, never ExCon's, reusable on any device, void after `TOKEN_DAYS` or a password reset) to `/inbox/go/<token>/`, which signs in and opens `/inbox/setup/` (the desk's `/inbox/desk/setup/`, `signin.setup_path`; alerts; on iPhone, Add to Home Screen); the manifest's `start_url` is that link (`?app=1`) for the signed-in person, as an iPhone's home-screen app doesn't share Safari's cookies, and sign-out sets `simex_signed_out` so the app doesn't sign itself back in. A person's `personal_email` (their own address; `Person.email` stays the email channel's) gets the email copies of an in-app inject with `Inject.also_email` (`render.build` adds `build_email(personal=True)` drafts, live only: test sends and runs leave them out) and their login email (`ops/logins.py`: a new password, the QR code inline as `cid:signin-qr`, a `Message` of kind `login` kept without the password or link; `Person.login_shared_at` / `_via`, also "by hand", cleared by any password reset; answers to `logins.FROM_LOCAL` are ignored); `ops/roster.py` imports a roster spreadsheet (plan, then apply) setting personal emails and teams and adding the rest. Participants' address books grow with use: `views_inbox.address_book` is who a mailbox may write to (checked on send), `contacts` the book shown (from the mailbox's own mail, latest first; empty at first), and anyone else is reached by their exact address (`lookup`, also `?key=` for a chip's name); leaders and the desk keep the full book. `ops/legacy_import.py` loads a backup from the first app. `ops/documents.py` matches an uploaded zip of inject documents in a thread of the web process (progress in `AttachmentImport`, shown on the Import page); tests set `documents.RUN_INLINE`. A follow-up (`delivery.send_followup`, inject page) is a `Message` of kind `followup` with `parent` = the live delivery it continues (in-app: same `Mail.thread`; email: `In-Reply-To` the parent's Message-ID, looked up from Resend at send time, queued for the scheduler); wherever live kinds are listed (`live`, `reply`) include `followup` and `leader`. A persona is identified by its username (`Sender.local_part`, `Message.persona`, `Mail.persona`, `Response.persona`, address-book key `p:<username>`), never by the address shown: `Sender.inapp_local` / `Sender.domain` only change its SIMEX Mail address (`render.persona_address` / `persona_addr`, so news@cnn.com and news@reuters.com can be two personas); email always sends from `<username>@MAIL_DOMAIN`. `ops/ai.py` (Claude analysis of replies: per-reply tags, rubric suggestions, exercise summary; Dashboard tab in `ops/views_ai.py`; plus `InjectReview`, a read of each unsent inject shown with the rule checks of `ops/checks.py` on the Injects list; and `DocumentAnalysis`, `ops/ai_docs.py`: each file a member or leader attached (SIMEX Mail Sent copies with their own data, never a forwarded inject document; email `ResponseAttachment`s), once per team and `sha256` (set on `MailAttachment` / `ResponseAttachment` save; pass it when copying), text and file facts from `ops/docscan.py` (no model; untrusted input, capped), found by `ai_docs.discover` on every AI tick including files sent before it existed, setting `ai_documents`; ExCon at `/documents/`, the reply page and Dashboard, leaders in the team inbox and their Dashboard, in reports' `ai_written` as entries with `document`; `ai_docs.prune` after clearing or withdrawing mail) runs in a thread of the scheduler worker, never in a request (the on-demand exercise summary and `InjectDraft`, Injects → Draft with AI, run in a thread of the web process); its suggestions are ExCon-only and never written to `Grade`. An `InjectDraft` (a new inject, a follow-up or a persona's answer, drafted from the injects, personas and replies) is never sent: ExCon makes it a `draft` inject (`AI-n`) or copies it into the follow-up or answer form. Control-side material for scenario-grounded drafting stays in `../undac-simex`, never here (the one exception is role play, below). `ops/report.py` compiles the end-of-exercise reports (Dashboard → Reports, `views_report.py`): a `Report` per team, written for the team, and one for ExCon (team None) in an `ExerciseReport`; figures are computed in code and snapshotted, Claude only writes the sections (ExCon edits them, browser print to PDF). A team report's input is that team's mail only, human grades only, and the AI-written estimate only if `include_ai_written`. It relies on `Inject.critical`, `RubricCriterion.critical` (must-have), `Message.ai_draft` / `ai_edited` (follow-ups and answers sent from an `InjectDraft`), read/download `Event`s and `MailAction` (what participants do in SIMEX Mail with its time: `views_inbox.track`, never for read-only views; log any new mailbox action there). Team-facing figures number the members instead of naming them (`report.for_team`). Provider is `AI_PROVIDER` (anthropic | bedrock | off); tests use `ai.set_client(fake)`. Dashboard → Network (`ops/network.py`, `views_network.py`, `static/ops/network.js`, a canvas force layout with no library; `?screen=1` the wall screen) maps who writes to whom, computed from what is kept, nothing stored: sent `Message`s (recipients from the mailboxes their Mail landed in, else team / person; never the leaders' inject copies, withdrawn, login or `rp` ones), participants' Sent `Mail` without a Message (`Mail.recipients`; the team inbox's copy of an answer from a member's own mailbox counted once) and email `Response`s without Mail; nodes `t:` `m:` `l:` `p:<username>` (the team leader persona is its leader), polled with a cursor; when a new way of sending mail is added, count it there. ExCon's Notifications panel (`ops/notices.py`, `base.html`, `app.js`) lists a `Notice` per event (unique `key`, so the same event never notifies twice), written where it happens (a reply in `inbound`, an AI flag, an inject failed / partly delivered / overdue in `delivery`, a finished draft, summary, report compile or document import, the scheduler stopping, checked in `excon_poll`); read and dismissed per ExCon user (`NoticeState`); `/excon/poll/` returns the list only when its `v` changed; add a notice for any new event ExCon should hear about. AI personas (`ops/npc.py`, `views_npc.py`, AI personas tab while Settings `npc_enabled`; `npc_scope` test | all) are personas (`NpcProfile` one-to-one with `Sender`, never the team leader persona or a `{team}` one) whose answers Claude writes in character: a team's `Response` to one queues an `NpcReply` (`npc.on_response`, from `inbound`) due after a delay drawn from its timing (or skipped), merged with later messages of the same conversation (`inbound.thread_key`); `npc.tick` in the scheduler's AI loop writes it when due and sends it with `delivery.answer(idem_key=npc-<id>, npc=True)` (`Message.npc`), or holds it for ExCon; profiles hold only participant-facing knowledge, are seeded from a persona's own injects (`npc.seed`, voice from all, knowledge never from planned ones) or a brief (`npc.generate`), and the sandbox (`NpcSandbox`) delivers nothing; team leaders see their team's conversations with them read-only at `/inbox/personas/` (`npc.team_conversations`). The persona desk (`ops/desk.py`; role `desk`, no team, `access.desk_of`, SIMEX Mail only, never ExCon staff) is one shared mailbox, Mail/MailLabel with team None and owner None (`Viewer` box `desk`, `/inbox/desk/`; ExCon may open it too): `desk.on_delivered` (from `delivery._put_mail` and a sent email) copies every persona delivery into its Sent and `desk.on_response` (from `inbound.from_mail` and a fetched email) every team message to a persona into its Inbox, with `Mail.desk_team` (the other side) and `Mail.desk_response`; never the team leader persona's, the leaders' copies, automatic answers or leaders' replies. In-app copies keep the team's thread id. `desk.send` answers a Response as the persona it went to (`delivery.answer(author=)`) or else sends a Message of kind `desk` to a team, one member (`m:<person id>` in the desk's book: in-app to their own mailbox, `Message.inbox_owner`, where the persona's answers to them go on) or one team leader (`l:<person id>`, kind `tl`, `views_inbox.desk_leaders`: to the leader's own mailbox, never the team's; the desk's Sent keeps it, as its only leader copies) (no inject: out of the injects' figures, listed with the live kinds for matching), needs live armed but for the Test team, and skips that AI persona's queued `NpcReply`. ExCon's Compose tab (`views_excon.compose`, `/compose/`) calls `desk.send` too (author None, "Sent by ExCon"). Personas are tagged by `Sender.colour` (`desk.colour`); presence via `desk.presence` (cache). Desk members read every team's mailboxes through the `/inbox/as/` views (Teams tab, `views_inbox.desk_teams`); the desk's reading never records `Event`s or `MailAction`s. Team-scoped Mail queries must keep their `team=` filter, as desk copies have none. Role play (`ops/roleplay.py`, plan in `web/docs/ROLEPLAY_PLAN.md`) coordinates the role players who play characters face to face, by phone or VTC: a `RolePlay` per MEL serial (`key`, `-b` for a second schedule row of one serial) with its sittings (`RolePlaySlot`: start, minutes, teams, none = all; status pending | ready | done | skipped), files (`RolePlayFile`) and players (Persons of role `rp`, "Role player (ExCon)", no team, `access.rp_of`; or team leaders, who play only their own team's sittings, `roleplay.slots_for` / `players_of`; or desk members), imported (`roleplay.read_workbook` / `records` / `plan` / `apply`) from the MEL-MIL's F2F/phone/VTC rows (script, outcomes) and a role-player schedule sheet (role, players), in one file or separately (a record carries only its sheets' fields, `MEL_ONLY` / `SCHEDULE_ONLY`), times parsed into per-team sittings (`parse_slots`), day names dated by `conf` `rp_days`. **It is the one place control-side material is kept** (`RolePlay.script`, `outcomes`, `notes`, `conf` `rp_briefing` / `rp_faq`): read only by ExCon's Role play tab (`views_roleplay.py`, `@excon_required`) and the role players assigned (`/inbox/rp/`, `views_rp.py`, `access.rp_required`, only their own sittings), never by participants' or leaders' mail views, the AI, AI personas, drafts, reports or injects (`tests/test_roleplay.py` scans those modules: keep it passing). The board polls `roleplay.version()` (`bump()` on any change); `roleplay.tick` (scheduler main loop) pushes reminders (`push.notify_people`) `rp_lead` minutes before and at each sitting's start, and "Changed" after a slip or move (`changed_at`), emails them (a `Message` of kind `rp`) to role players without push if `rp_email`, and adds an `rp-late:<slot>` Notice when nobody is in position. QR sign-in sends role players to `/inbox/rp/setup/`; leaders with role plays get a My role plays tab. Start afresh calls `roleplay.reset` (sittings pending at `imported_start`). A delivery sent by mistake is withdrawn on its Log page (`delivery.withdraw`, `/log/<id>/withdraw/`): every Mail of that Message (and, for a live inject, that team's leader copy) in the chosen teams' mailboxes and the desk's is deleted, the Message becomes `withdrawn` (out of the `sent` figures); teams' replies stay. `ops/reset.py` clears test traffic: Team inboxes → Clear mailboxes (a team or all, every mailbox or members' own only, optionally its Log deliveries) and Settings → Start afresh (all exercise activity, sent injects back on the schedule; sets `conf` `reset_at`, before which `inbound.sync` ignores received email); add any new activity model there. The simulation clock (`ops/simclock.py`, Settings → Simulation clock, `conf` `sim_*`: an anchor, a speed, optionally following live injects' `scenario_dt` forward) stamps each new `Mail` with `sim_at` (`Mail.save`; an inject's own delivery takes its `scenario_dt`, the rest the clock's time; copies keep the source's, so pass `sim_at` when copying a Mail by hand); SIMEX Mail shows members only that time (`views_inbox.sees_sim_only`; template tags `listdate` / `msgdate` / `detaildates`, context `sim` from `ops.context.sim`) and leaders, the desk and ExCon both, with a clock widget in `templates/ops/mail/base.html` kept in step by `/poll/`'s `clock`; never show a member a real date in SIMEX Mail. The repo is AGPL-3.0 (`LICENSE`): every page of ops and SIMEX Mail, sign-in pages included, links to the source code (`settings.SOURCE_URL`, `ops.context.source`); keep that link on any new base template. The directory is `web/`, not `django/`, so it doesn't shadow the django package.

The text below describes the first version (FastAPI, `app/`), kept for rollback via `.do/app-fastapi.yaml`.

FastAPI + Jinja (server-rendered, no build step) + SQLAlchemy 2 (Postgres on DO, SQLite locally) web app at https://ops.simex.kobolabs.dev that schedules and sends UNDAC SIMEX email injects via Resend (personas at @simex.kobolabs.dev, which also receives), and tracks and grades participants' replies. README.md is the human-facing version of this file; keep them in sync. `docs/MANUAL.md` is the operator manual (served at /help): update it whenever a screen, label or flow changes.

- `app/main.py` routes (tabs: Injects, Test runs, Senders, People & teams, Log, Settings), auth (single login from env), CSRF origin check. New tools go in as new tabs.
- `app/db.py` models; `app/importer.py` MEL/JSON import (participant-facing fields only, plan/apply with local-edit tracking); `app/render.py` inject -> concrete emails (placeholders, footer, test redirect); `app/scheduler.py` loop + live/test/one-off sending; `app/mailer.py` Resend client (idempotency keys) and log mailer; `app/settings.py` DB-backed operator settings.
- `app/inbound.py` Resend webhook (Svix signature), fetch of received emails, matching to message/inject/team (reply-to token `persona+token@`, else a guess); grading (`RubricCriterion`, `Grade`) and the Replies tab are in `main.py`.
- Tests: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q` (SQLite, log mailer, scheduler off; `tick()` is called directly).

Rules:
- **Never store control-side material** (expected outcomes, story, theme, key figures, relevance, MEL description). The importer drops them; keep it that way. The one exception is role play (`RolePlay` script, outcomes, notes and the role players' briefing), walled off as described above. No exercise content in git (`*.xlsx`, `injects*.json` are ignored).
- Every email gets a `Message` row before it is sent, with a deterministic idempotency key; keep that order so a crash mid-send never duplicates.
- Live sends require `live_armed`; `paused` stops everything; scheduled injects past the grace period become `overdue`, never auto-sent. Placeholder addresses (`.invalid`, example domains) are stripped from live sends.
- Times are naive UTC in the DB, shown and entered in `EXERCISE_TZ` (Africa/Nairobi).
- One app instance only (in-process scheduler). Secrets are not in `.do/app.yaml`; use `.do/render_spec.py` before `doctl apps update`.
- Pushing to `main` deploys to production: run the tests first.
